# Abel

Abel is an AI receptionist for trades businesses — plumbing, HVAC, electrical and roofing — built for owner-operators and small teams who miss calls while they're on a job.

## Key Focus Areas

- Answers calls using the business's own hours, service area, pricing and job types
- Books, reschedules and cancels against live calendar availability
- Texts confirmations, reminders, schedule changes and payment links (with customer consent)
- Logs calls and captures customer details and job notes for the owner
