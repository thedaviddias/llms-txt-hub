# SpeedPainter

SpeedPainter creates hand-drawn videos in the browser across two core tools sharing one account and credit balance: Explainer Video (script to narrated whiteboard explainer animation) and Speedpaint (image to hand-drawn sketch reveal video).

## Key Focus Areas

- **AI Whiteboard Explainer Video**: Turns a written script or prompt into a multi-scene narrated whiteboard video with voiceover, subtitles, and 16:9, 9:16, or 1:1 exports.
- **Image-to-Speedpaint Generator**: Converts photos, illustrations, or logos into hand-drawn sketch-and-color fill reveal videos in MP4, WebM, or GIF.
- **Browser-Based Workflow**: No desktop software installation required.

## About llms.txt Implementation

SpeedPainter provides structured `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt` documentation detailing both video engines, supported aspect ratios, export formats, credit rules, and direct tool URLs for AI assistants and agents.
