# App Store Tracker

App Store Tracker monitors iOS App Store top free rankings, app metadata and ranking history across 114 storefronts and all 22 categories, updated daily\.

## Key Focus Areas

- **Ranking history**: Daily top free chart history since 2025-03-09, by country and category.
- **App and developer pages**: Ratings, pricing, screenshots, availability and rank history per app.
- **Design library**: Captured app screens, flows and UI patterns.
