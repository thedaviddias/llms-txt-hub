# Pho Chat

AI research workspace for clinicians and researchers\. Answers cite real PubMed and DOI sources, and unsourced claims are labelled\. Bring your own API key\.

## About

Phở Chat is an AI workspace for research and clinical questions. Answers are grounded on real literature from PubMed, Crossref and OpenAlex, with citations inside the text and evidence levels. When no source backs a claim, it is labelled **unverified** instead of given a made-up reference.

## Features

- **Cited answers:** every claim links to a real PubMed or DOI source
- **Systematic review screening:** AI screens titles and abstracts against your criteria, you make the final call
- **Deep research reports** exported to Word, with references in RIS
- **PRISMA diagrams** built from your screening decisions
- **Bring your own key** for OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Groq, DeepSeek, xAI or OpenRouter, no markup
- Interface in 14 languages

## Getting Started

1. Sign up free at `https://pho.chat`
2. Ask a research or clinical question and check the sources beside the answer
3. Optional: add your own API key in Settings to use any model you like

The llms.txt file is at `https://pho.chat/llms.txt`
