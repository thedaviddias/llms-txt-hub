# La Demeure des Dix

La Demeure des Dix is a fully privatized holiday house for up to 10 guests in Elbeuf, about 20 minutes from Rouen in Normandy, France: 280 m², five king-size bedrooms, a private heated swim spa, an outdoor sauna and an enclosed garden of about 1,500 m². Dogs are accepted with prior host approval (QUALIDOG label).
