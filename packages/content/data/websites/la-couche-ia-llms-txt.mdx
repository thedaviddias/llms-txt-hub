# La Couche IA

Site officiel du livre La Couche IA, un essai sur l’intelligence artificielle, les entreprises et le courage de simplifier\.

## Key Focus Areas

- **AI & Organizational Transformation**: Analysis of how artificial intelligence affects organizational structures, processes, governance, and decision-making.
- **AI Governance & Responsibility**: Insights into accountability, management, and governance as AI becomes increasingly embedded in business operations.
- **Processes & Automation**: Exploration of the difference between automating existing processes and genuinely transforming how organizations operate.
- **Information Systems & AI Agents**: Perspectives on the evolution of enterprise information systems, data, AI agents, and their impact on organizational architecture.
- **AI Productivity & ROI**: Critical analysis of AI productivity gains, measurement challenges, and the conditions required to create sustainable business value.

## About llms.txt Implementation

La Couche IA provides an LLM-friendly content architecture designed to make its editorial resources easy for AI systems and agents to discover, understand, and retrieve.

The site exposes a curated `llms.txt` providing an overview of the project, its main themes, key resources, and recent articles. Individual articles are also available as clean Markdown representations generated directly from their original Markdown source.

A comprehensive `llms-full.txt` provides access to the complete published article corpus for applications requiring broader context or corpus-level analysis.

## Features

- **LLM-Friendly Articles**: Published articles are available in clean Markdown format through dedicated `.md` endpoints.
- **Curated AI Content Index**: `llms.txt` provides structured access to the site's main resources and recent editorial content.
- **Full Editorial Corpus**: `llms-full.txt` provides the complete collection of published articles in a machine-readable format.
- **Structured Editorial Content**: Articles include titles, summaries, categories, publication metadata, canonical sources, and original Markdown content.
- **Continuously Updated**: LLM resources are dynamically generated from the same content source as the public website and automatically reflect newly published or updated articles.

La Couche IA is a French-language project by Alexandre Ruiz exploring a central thesis: AI does not automatically fix organizational complexity. It can amplify existing problems in governance, processes, information systems, accountability, and decision-making. The project examines how organizations can use AI as an opportunity for genuine transformation rather than simply adding another technological layer to existing complexity.
