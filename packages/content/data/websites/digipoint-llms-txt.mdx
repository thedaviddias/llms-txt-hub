# DigiPoint

DigiPoint is a digital subscription and software marketplace in Bangladesh offering AI tools, developer IDEs, creative software, streaming services, and VPN subscriptions with local BDT payments (bKash, Nagad, Rocket) and crypto checkout.

## Key Focus Areas

- **Structured Live Pricing**: Exposes `/pricing.md` and `/price-list` with live USD and approximate BDT checkout rates, stock availability, and plan durations.
- **Buyer Decision Utilities**: Provides side-by-side subscription comparison matrices (`/compare`), profession-curated stacks (`/for`), and an interactive bank dual-currency card (+15% VAT) vs. bKash calculator.
- **Automated Fulfillment & Reseller API**: Supports 24/7 automatic delivery on API/redeem-code items alongside an authenticated REST API (`/api/reseller/v1`).

## About llms.txt Implementation

DigiPoint publishes `/llms.txt` as a concise index of categories, products, comparisons, and guides, alongside `/llms-full.txt` containing complete variant-level pricing, stock status, and delivery details.
