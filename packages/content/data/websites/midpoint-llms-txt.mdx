# Midpoint

Trading card prices, grading and centering for 1.5M+ Pokémon, Magic, Yu-Gi-Oh!, One Piece, Lorcana and sports cards: raw and graded market values from real sold listings, AI grading, centering measurement, grading ROI and weekly market research.

## Key Focus Areas

- Trading card prices (raw and PSA, CGC, BGS, SGC, TAG graded)
- Card grading ROI and centering
- Weekly market research reports with downloadable data
- Read-only MCP server for AI agents (mcp.cardcenteringtool.com)

## About llms.txt Implementation

Midpoint provides an `llms.txt` file and a detailed `llms-full.txt` describing the app, the price database, the market research reports, the MCP server and structured endpoints for AI agents.
