# HireLayer

HireLayer gives developers and HR-tech teams recruiting APIs. Resume Parse turns PDF, Word, scanned or photographed CVs into structured JSON. Job Extract turns a job description into weighted criteria. Match and Rank score candidates against a job with an explanation for each criterion, and Skills normalizes free-text skills to a taxonomy.

## Key Focus Areas

- Resume and CV Parsing
- Job Criteria Extraction
- Candidate Matching and Ranking
- Skills Normalization
