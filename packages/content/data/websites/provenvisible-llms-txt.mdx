# ProvenVisible

Tracks whether ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews mention and cite your brand\. SEO, AEO and GEO audits with fixes verified on the live site\.

## What ProvenVisible does

- **AI answer tracking**: asks ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews the questions your customers ask, every week, and records whether your brand is mentioned and which sources are cited.
- **SEO, AEO and GEO audits**: 126 deterministic checks, including AI crawler access and llms.txt.
- **Fix and verify**: concrete changes applied through a WordPress plugin, a Git pull request or a PHP agent, then re-checked on the live site.
- **Free tools**: a robots.txt and llms.txt checker at `https://provenvisible.com/crawlers`

## About our llms.txt

Our llms.txt lists the product pages, pricing, free tools and blog posts (in English, Turkish, Spanish, German and French) so assistants can find the right page for a question.
