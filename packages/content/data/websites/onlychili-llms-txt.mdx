# OnlyChili

OnlyChili is an AI-native creator streaming platform dedicated to serialized AI short dramas, multi-episode playlists, vertical Shorts, and independent creator channels powered by AI Chili.

## Key Focus Areas

- **Serialized AI Cinema & Micro-Dramas**: Public multi-episode series playlists, vertical Shorts, and genre channels across drama, sci-fi, fantasy, anime, and photorealistic cinema in English and French.
- **Direct Creator Monetization**: Filmmakers publish from AI Chili Studio and monetize through recurring channel memberships and per-episode pay-per-view unlocks.
- **Agent & Search Native Discovery**: Live `llms.txt`, `llms-full.txt`, real-time WebSub RSS (`/rss.xml`), and a public read-only Model Context Protocol endpoint (`/mcp`).

## About llms.txt Implementation

OnlyChili serves both a concise `llms.txt` index and an expanded `llms-full.txt` catalog summary dynamically generated from live public series, episodes, genres, and MCP tool schemas.
