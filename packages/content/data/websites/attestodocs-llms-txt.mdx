# ATTESTODOCS

Certified translations for USCIS and U\.S\. immigration filings: $24\.95 per page side, no word cap, 24\-hour delivery for up to 10 pages\.

## About Attesto

Attesto is an online service for certified document translation for U.S. immigration. Upload a document, see a locked price before you pay, and receive a certified English translation with the signed statement of accuracy and translator competence that 8 CFR 103.2(b)(3) requires for documents submitted to USCIS.

## Key facts

- **Price:** $24.95 per physical page side, with no word count and no word cap
- **Languages:** 80 languages into English, including Spanish, Arabic, Portuguese, French, Ukrainian, Russian, Chinese, Hindi, Vietnamese, Tagalog, Korean and Persian
- **Turnaround:** 24 hours for up to 10 pages, plus 24 hours per further 10 pages; expedite (+50%) roughly halves it
- **Add-ons:** notarization $19.95 (USCIS does not require it), physical copy by mail $14.95, apostille $99.00
- **Package Check:** free cross-document check of names, dates and places on multi-document orders
- **RFE Risk Scan:** free check of a translation you already have against the USCIS rules
- **Guarantee:** if USCIS issues a Request for Evidence because of our translation, a full refund and a free re-translation

## What the llms.txt covers

The [llms.txt](https://attestodocs.com/llms.txt) maps the site for language models: how an order works, pricing, the guarantee, Package Check and the RFE Risk Scan; guides to the USCIS translation rules (certified vs. notarized, page counting, name and date mismatches, answering an RFE); translation requirements for 20 USCIS and consular forms (I-130, I-485, N-400, I-751, DS-260 and more); and immigration use cases from green cards and asylum to employment visas.
