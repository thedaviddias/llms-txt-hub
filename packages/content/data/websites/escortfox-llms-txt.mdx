# EscortFox

EscortFox is an international escort directory covering more than 1,000 cities. Independent escorts, escort agencies and venues are listed by country and city, each with photos, rates, services and direct contact details. Advertisers create and manage their own listings, and profiles can be verified with a photo check.

## Key Focus Areas

- Escort, agency and venue listings by country and city
- Profile verification
- Guides and safety advice for clients and advertisers
- Installable web app with passkey sign-in

## About llms.txt Implementation

EscortFox's llms.txt explains the site's URL scheme (language prefixes, `/escorts/{COUNTRY}/{city}` location pages and `{slug}-{id}` listing pages) and links the main browse pages, the guides, the advertiser pages and the policies, plus the sitemap.
