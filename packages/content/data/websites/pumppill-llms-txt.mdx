# PumpPill

PumpPill indexes Robinhood Chain \(an Arbitrum Orbit L2, chain ID 4663\) and Solana\. It reads a token contract and reports what is wrong with it, and keeps a record of what happened to tokens after they launched\.

## Key Focus Areas

- **Token safety reads**: holder concentration, bundled-launch detection, deployer history and whether a token can still be sold.
- **Measured outcomes**: counts measured forward from the market cap at the moment each token was logged, with losers in the denominator.
- **Agent access**: a read-only MCP server at `https://api.pumppill.org/mcp`, listed in the official MCP Registry as `org.pumppill/token-safety`.

## About llms.txt Implementation

The llms.txt file lists quotable figures with their method and limits, points assistants to the live JSON that carries its own timestamp, and explains how to cite PumpPill. llms-full.txt carries the longer reference.
