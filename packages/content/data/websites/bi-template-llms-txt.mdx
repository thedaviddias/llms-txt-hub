# BI Template

BI Template sells Power BI report templates for finance, HR, sales, and marketing teams: P&L, portfolio monitoring, sales pipeline, HR, digital marketing, and Meta and Google Ads reports. Each template comes from a client project at Datapilot, a data consulting firm, and ships with a step-by-step deployment guide and an anonymized dataset, at a fixed price with no subscription.

## Key Focus Areas

- Power BI templates by department, each with an interactive demo
- A deployment kit with every template: the Power BI report (.pbix), a deployment guide (PDF), and an anonymized dataset (Excel)
- Free Power BI resources sent by email, including the Power BI Starter Kit
- Power BI consulting by the hour or the half day
