# ObraVision

ObraVision is a digital construction daily log (RDO, diário de obra) for Brazilian contractors. Teams record the day in the browser or by telling it on WhatsApp in text or audio; the manager approves, three parties sign and the branded PDF goes to the client right away.

## Key Focus Areas

- Daily log by WhatsApp text or audio, or in the browser
- Approval and three-party signature with branded PDF
- Templates for civil works, electrical installation (NBR 5410, NR-10) and fire alarm systems (NBR 17240)
- SPT soil testing, foundations and piles in the same project

## About llms.txt Implementation

ObraVision publishes `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt` (in Portuguese) with features, pricing, FAQ and an honest comparison page.
