# Music Genie

Music Genie is an AI music generation platform for video creators that generates copyright\-safe background music matched to video mood, pacing, and scene changes\.

## Key Focus Areas

- **Video-to-Music Synchronization**: Analyzes uploaded video motion pacing, color mood, and scene transitions to synthesize matched original soundtracks.
- **Text-to-Music Synthesis**: Generates original royalty-free tracks from natural language prompts specifying genre, tempo, and instrumentation.
- **Creator Audio Utilities**: Includes browser-based tools for AI music detection and audio trimming, plus stem export for generated music.

The Music Genie `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt` indexes provide structured documentation covering video-to-music generation capabilities, audio utility routes, and commercial licensing specifications.
