# NLocale

NLocale is a public local guide to the Aisne department in France, published by SASU JAK MOOH: a dated events calendar, documented places and heritage, restaurants, places to stay, local authorities and editorial articles, with original sources cited.

## Key Focus Areas

- Sourced events calendar and weekend picks across Aisne
- Documented places, heritage, restaurants and stays
- Information for the 797 municipalities and local authorities of Aisne
- Editorial articles in French, English, German, Dutch, Italian and Spanish

## About llms.txt Implementation

The llms.txt and llms-full.txt describe the publisher, the main public sections, the latest published articles and citation rules (distinguishing source dates, consultation, publication and dated visits). A public read-only MCP endpoint with ten search and read tools is documented on the site.
