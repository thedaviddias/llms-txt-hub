## QuantaVal

Quantitative valuation engine — 88 models, one fair value.

QuantaVal is a quantitative valuation engine for stocks, ETFs, crypto, metals and FX. Its Quantitative Valuation Engine runs 88 deterministic valuation models — DCF, reverse DCF, multiples, residual income and more — converging into one transparent fair-value range. The free calculator offers 62 models with no registration: visitors get 3 shared calculations a day, resetting at midnight UTC, no credit card required, and the full methodology is free to read. Includes a free stock screener covering 2,000+ stocks, an equity research terminal, and 78 published case studies.
