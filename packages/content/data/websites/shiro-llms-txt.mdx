# Shiro

Shiro is a book-writing app for novels and non-fiction in English and French. Writers plan on a Board of cards, write on a quiet page, keep characters and places in World or sources in Research, and export EPUB and print files from Publish. The AI is optional, and a hosted MCP server lets agents work on a book with every change kept in the project's History.

## Key Focus Areas

- Novel and non-fiction writing
- Outlining on cards, plot grids and timelines
- Story bible, continuity and research
- EPUB and print formatting
- Optional AI and a hosted MCP server
