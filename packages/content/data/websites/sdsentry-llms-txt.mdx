# SDSentry

OSHA HazCom and Safety Data Sheet software for small, multi\-site employers: per\-location SDS library, barcode lookup, GHS labels and training records\.

## What it does

- Per-location SDS library workers can open on a phone
- Scan a product barcode to pull up its SDS
- GHS secondary-container label maker
- Per-chemical HazCom training with e-signature records
- Written HazCom program builder

## Free tools

SDS search, GHS label maker and a 12-question HazCom self-audit, no sign-up.
