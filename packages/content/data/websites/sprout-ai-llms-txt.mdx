# Sprout AI

Sprout AI is a read-only AI personal finance app by MindCrafters LLC. It connects to eligible US and Canadian bank, credit union and card accounts through Plaid and answers plain-English questions about spending, subscriptions, cash flow and unusual charges, naming the accounts and date range behind every figure. It runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac and the web, and inside Claude and other MCP clients through a remote MCP server.

## Key Focus Areas

- AI Expense Tracking
- Subscription and Recurring Charge Detection
- Cash Flow and Spending Analysis
- MCP Server for AI Assistants
