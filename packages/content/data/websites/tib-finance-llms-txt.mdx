# TIB Finance

Montreal\-based payment platform and REST API: Interac, pre\-authorized debit \(PAD\), EFT, instant deposits and cards, for businesses and software platforms\.

## About TIB Finance

TIB Finance is a payment platform founded in 2018 in Montreal. Businesses and software platforms use it to collect and send payments through a web portal or a REST API.

## Payment methods

- **Interac**: e-Transfer and Request Money
- **Pre-authorized debit (PAD)**
- **EFT**: electronic funds transfers between bank accounts
- **Instant deposits**
- **Credit cards**
