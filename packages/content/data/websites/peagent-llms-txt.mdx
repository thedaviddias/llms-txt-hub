# PEARL AGENT (PEAGENT)

PEARL AGENT ($PEAGENT) is a four-day, Agent-ID-gated claim season token on BNB Chain. Every claim opens an oyster: a blockhash draw inside the claim contract pays 80% 4,000 PEAGENT (Common Pearl), 15% 8,000 (White Pearl), or 5% 20,000 (GOLDEN PEARL). Claims 1-2 each UTC day cost 0.0002 BNB and claims 3-5 cost 0.0005 BNB, with a maximum of 5 claims per day and 20 per wallet. The season ends with a listing funded by locked claim fees, with the LP burned. Claim season ends November 14, 2026, at 12:26 UTC.
