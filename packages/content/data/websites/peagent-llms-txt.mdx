# PEARL AGENT (PEAGENT)

PEARL AGENT ($PEAGENT) is a four-day, Agent-ID-gated claim season token on BNB Chain. Each claim opens an oyster: a blockhash draw inside the claim contract pays 80% 4,000 PEAGENT (Common Pearl), 15% 8,000 (White Pearl), or 5% 20,000 (GOLDEN PEARL). Claims 1-2 per UTC day cost 0.0002 BNB, claims 3-5 cost 0.0005 BNB (max 5/day, 20/wallet). After the season ends Nov 14, 2026, 12:26 UTC, listing needs someone to call launch() and at least one claim; locked claim fees fund the listing, LP burned.
