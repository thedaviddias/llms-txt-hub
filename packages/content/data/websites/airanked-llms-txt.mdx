# AI Ranked

AI Ranked is a publication about AI search. It covers how ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot and Google AI Overviews retrieve and cite sources, with every factual claim linked to a primary source.

## Key Focus Areas

- **Guides**: GEO, AEO, measuring AI visibility, AI crawler user agents and llms.txt.
- **Research**: an original study of AI crawler access in the robots.txt files of 261 major sites, plus sourced AI search statistics.
- **Free tools**: an llms.txt generator and a robots.txt AI crawler checker.

## About llms.txt Implementation

The llms.txt file lists the site's guides, research, glossary, news and buyer's guides, each with a one-line summary, so agents can find the right page quickly.
