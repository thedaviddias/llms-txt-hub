# Sequential

Session replay and product analytics with an AI judge that reviews each session and lists the findings to fix\.

## Key Focus Areas

- **Session replay**: records and replays what people do in your product, on the web and on mobile.
- **AI judge**: reviews each session for what the user came to do, whether they got there and where they struggled.
- **Product analytics**: events, funnels, flows and retention.
- **Agent access**: a remote MCP server lets AI agents query sessions, findings, events, funnels, flows and retention.
