# MarkenTIQ

French-language open lab on B2B marketing and AI. It publishes methods and skills packs in the open, free to use with a simple attribution, and sells nothing from the site.

## What the llms.txt covers

- **Four skills packs**: GEO (visibility in AI answers), Positioning & Business Story, SEO Skills, Marketing Skills
- **Five step-by-step methods**, each stating its protocol and its limits rather than a promised result
- **Five complete worked examples**, including a real audit of our own site rather than a fictional case
- **A 59-term glossary**: ten terms have a dedicated page, the other 49 are listed by anchor, so a model can reach one definition without loading a 55 KB page
- **A ten-episode public build log**, including the measurements that came back at zero

## Notes on the implementation

The file is written by hand, not generated: every entry carries a one-line description. A control compares it to the sitemap before each publication and fails if a published page is missing from the index, or if a listed link is not declared in the sitemap. No llms-full.txt yet.
