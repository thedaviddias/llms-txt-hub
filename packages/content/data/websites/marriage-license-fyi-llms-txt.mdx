# MarriageLicense.fyi

Free directory of marriage licence and marriage notice offices across GB, IE, NZ, AU, CA and US. Find the office that issues your licence, with requirements, fees, hours and appointment links.

## What MarriageLicense.fyi offers

- **1,400+ issuing offices**: country, region and city pages listing the clerk or registrar office that issues marriage licences, with address, phone, hours and fees, sourced from public registers.
- **Requirements and guides**: what documents to bring, waiting periods, expiration windows and officiant rules per jurisdiction.
- **MCP server and public API**: read-only tools (`search_listings`, `get_listing`, `list_places`, `list_categories`, `get_stats`, `list_sources`) at `https://marriagelicense.fyi/mcp` for AI assistants.

## About the llms.txt

The llms.txt summarizes the directory scope, the URL structure (country → region → city → office), and how AI assistants should use the MCP tools to answer marriage licence questions.
