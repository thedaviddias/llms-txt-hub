# MarriageLicense.fyi

Free US directory of marriage license offices. Find the county clerk office that issues your license, with requirements, fees, hours, and appointment links for every state and county.

## What MarriageLicense.fyi offers

- **Every US marriage license office**: state, county, and city pages listing the clerk or recorder office that issues marriage licenses, with address, phone, hours, and fees.
- **Requirements and guides**: what documents to bring, waiting periods, expiration windows, and officiant rules per state.
- **MCP server and public API**: read-only tools (`search_listings`, `get_listing`, `list_places`, `list_categories`, `get_stats`, `list_sources`) at `https://marriagelicense.fyi/mcp` for AI assistants.

## About the llms.txt

The llms.txt summarizes the directory scope, the URL structure (state → county → city → office), and how AI assistants should use the MCP tools to answer marriage license questions.
