# BKK Staff

Automated resume screening for small businesses: post jobs, rank applicants, email candidates\.

## Key Focus Areas

- **Application screening**: Reads every application (CV or a short phone quiz for roles where people rarely have a resume) and leaves a shortlist to interview.
- **Candidate email**: Sends polite declines and follow-up questions in the candidate's language.
- **MCP server**: Remote Streamable HTTP endpoint at https://app.bkkstaff.com/mcp with OAuth 2.1 and PKCE, for an employer's AI assistant.
