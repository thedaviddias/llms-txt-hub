# Japanese Language Consultancy

Japanese to English and English to Japanese translation and interpretation for technical, legal, medical and business documents\. Based in Bengaluru since 2010\.

## About Japanese Language Consultancy

Japanese Language Consultancy is a translation and interpretation firm based in Bengaluru, India, working between Japanese and English since 2010. The team has eight translators and three interpreters.

## Services

- **Translation**: Japanese to English and English to Japanese
- **Editing and proofreading**: editing against the source, target-language proofreading and back translation
- **Interpretation**: consecutive and simultaneous, on site; consecutive also by phone
- **Interpretation languages**: Japanese with English, Kannada, Tamil and Hindi

## Subject Areas

- **Technical**: manuals, specifications, engineering drawings, automotive, electronics, IT and software
- **Legal**: contracts, corporate documents, certified official documents
- **Patents**: specifications, claims and prior art
- **Medical and pharmaceutical**: papers and documents
- **Business**: financial, corporate, marketing and website content
