# MEDIAPRONET

MEDIAPRONET is a curated directory where founders list new startups, SaaS tools and indie products. Each listing has its own page with a description, category, tech stack, screenshots, reviews and an outbound link. The site also publishes founder guides, including a guide on where to promote a startup.

## Key Focus Areas

- Startup and Product Discovery
- SaaS and Indie Product Listings
- Founder Guides
- Launch and Distribution Resources

## About llms.txt Implementation

MEDIAPRONET's llms.txt explains how listings are structured and links the browse, trending, category and tag pages, the guides and articles, the about and pricing pages, and the MCP server details for AI agents. The llms-full.txt is a complete index of approved listings grouped by category, and it also points agents to the read-only MCP endpoint.
