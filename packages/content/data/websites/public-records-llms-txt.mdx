# Public Records

Public Records publishes 18 Apify Actors for official public-records and public-web datasets. Agents can call them over the Apify API or via hosted MCP (`https://mcp.apify.com?tools=publicrecords/<actor>`). Runs bill through the user's own Apify account (pay-per-use).

## Key Focus Areas

- US federal grants (Grants.gov) and contract/grant awards (USAspending, SAM.gov)
- EU TED and UK Find a Tender procurement notices
- City council meetings and documents on Legistar
- New job postings from Ashby, Greenhouse, Lever and SmartRecruiters boards
- Company social profile links, Google Ads Transparency, Etsy sales, sports odds, Spotify play counts
