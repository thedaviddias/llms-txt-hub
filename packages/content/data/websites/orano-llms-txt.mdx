# ORANO

ORANO turns saved Reels, TikToks, YouTube videos, articles and PDFs into searchable summaries, key takeaways and step-by-step plans. It is available on iOS, Android and the web, and it also offers a personal, read-only MCP connection so ChatGPT, Claude or Cursor can search a user's saved library.

## Key Focus Areas

- Saving videos, posts, articles and PDFs from the share sheet or a pasted link
- Searchable summaries and key takeaways
- Ordered steps or project plans for recipes, workouts and tutorials
- Read-only MCP access to a personal saved library
