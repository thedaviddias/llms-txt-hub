# Yuboraman

Yuboraman\.uz routes Facebook and Instagram leads into CRMs, Telegram, Google Sheets and Uzbek CPA affiliate networks, in real time and without code\.

## Key Focus Areas

- Real-time delivery of Meta Lead Ads leads through Meta's signed webhook
- Up to five destinations per form: Bitrix24, amoCRM/Kommo, Google Sheets, HTTP endpoints and CPA networks
- Telegram mirroring of every lead to chats, groups, channels or forum topics
- Phone numbers in Facebook and Instagram comments captured as leads
- Interface in Uzbek, Russian and English
