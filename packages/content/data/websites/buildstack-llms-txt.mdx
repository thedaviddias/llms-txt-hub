# BuildStack

BuildStack is a free ranked directory of AI tools. You type a use case and get best-fit tools, with dated checks against official pricing pages.

## Key Focus Areas

- Use-case search for AI tools
- Editorial ranking (ranks are not for sale)
- Machine-readable /llms.txt, /llms-full.txt, and /tools.json

## About llms.txt Implementation

BuildStack publishes /llms.txt and /llms-full.txt at the site root, plus a full catalog at /tools.json.
