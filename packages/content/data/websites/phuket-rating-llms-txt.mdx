# Phuket Rating

Independent ratings of service companies in Phuket, Thailand, published one rating per sector (yacht charter, helicopter and jet charter). Companies are scored 0-100 on eight published criteria and every fact carries a source link. The llms.txt file lists each rated company with its score, so an assistant can answer a ranking question without crawling the whole site.
