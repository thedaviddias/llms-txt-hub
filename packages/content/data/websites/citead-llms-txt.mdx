# Citead

Citead is a Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) platform. It scores a web page from 0 to 100 on how easily language models can understand, reuse and cite it, explains each criterion with its evidence and lists the fixes to apply. A higher score makes a page easier to cite; it does not guarantee a citation.

## Key Focus Areas

- **Free GEO score**: paste a URL or a text and get the score, the score of each criterion and the fixes, with no account.
- **Public MCP server**: `https://mcp.citead.com/public/mcp/`, no API key, tools `score_url` and `score_text` for AI agents.
- **AI citation tracking**: tracked prompts are checked every week on ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews, with citation rate per engine and competitor comparison.
- **Public benchmark**: a public leaderboard of websites ranked by GEO score.
