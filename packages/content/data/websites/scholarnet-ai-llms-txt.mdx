# ScholarNet AI

Free AI study tools for college students: a thesis statement guide and generator for each essay type, plus an AI tutor, quizzes and flashcards\.

## What it does

- Thesis statement pages for argumentative, analytical, compare-and-contrast, expository and research essays
- Thesis generator that suggests a thesis and alternatives to adapt, not submit
- AI tutor, quiz and flashcard generators

Free, no account needed for the thesis tools.
