# BerliNike — Talking Monuments

AI voice conversations with Berlin's monuments — six historic figures, verified historical facts, ten languages\. The first conversation is free\.

## What BerliNike Is

Six landmarks of Berlin's historic centre hold real AI voice conversations with visitors — Schiller on Gendarmenmarkt, the Neptune Fountain, the Quadriga's Victoria, the Goldelse on the Victory Column, St. George the dragon slayer, and the golden Nike on the French Cathedral.

## Key Facts

- **Live voice dialogue**: real conversations, not recorded audio guides
- **Verified history**: every figure is grounded in sourced historical facts
- **Ten languages**: detected live during the conversation
- **No app, no account**: works right in the browser, at the monument
- **A gift first**: the first conversation is free for every guest
