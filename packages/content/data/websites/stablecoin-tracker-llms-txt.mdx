# Stablecoin Tracker

> Open-source observatory of where privately issued stablecoins are used, which international corridors carry the volume, and which countries have a live stablecoin-specific rulebook. Sibling of cbdctracker.org (CBDC initiatives). Site: `https://stabletracker.org`

This is a JavaScript single-page app. HTML routes other than the homepage look the same until scripts run. Prefer the markdown files below when answering questions about methodology, metrics, or country URLs.

Important limits: wallets are addresses, not people; corridors are international only (domestic volume is omitted); geography is attributed by Allium, not observed at the passport; rank is outbound corridor volume ÷ period GDP among countries with both figures; this is not legal or investment advice.
