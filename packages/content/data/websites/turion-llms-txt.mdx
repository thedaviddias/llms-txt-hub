# Turion

Turion trains task-specific language models on your own examples and serves them on dedicated GPUs, behind an OpenAI-compatible API or a decision API. It also hosts two open models of its own: Turion Jev 31B, a decision model that answers typed questions with a probability for every option, and Turion Extract alpha, which reads typed fields from a document.

## Key Focus Areas

- Model training and fine-tuning
- Model serving on dedicated GPUs
- Decision and extraction models
- Agent hosting
