# ClearFile

AI-powered credit repair service designed to help consumers dispute errors on their credit reports from Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion.

## Key Focus Areas

- AI-driven credit dispute automation
- Compliance with CROA regulations
- Affordable pricing starting at $29/month
- Automated tracking and letter generation

## About llms.txt Implementation

ClearFile provides an `llms.txt` file with AI-readable information about its credit repair service, including pricing tiers (Starter $29/mo, Standard $49/mo, Priority $89/mo), compliance posture, and service description.

The platform uses machine learning to analyze credit reports, identify inaccuracies, and automate dispute processes - making credit building more accessible and affordable.
