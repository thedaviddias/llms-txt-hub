# The Persuasion Taxonomy

The Persuasion Taxonomy is a public reference work for persuasive writing, published by Coppica under CC BY 4.0. It catalogs 766 techniques documented from real ads, sales letters, emails and pages, each with a stable ID, a definition, real examples and notes on when it lands and when it backfires. It's organized by the nine questions every reader silently asks.

## Key Focus Areas

- Persuasion techniques with real advertising examples
- Copywriting and conversion research
- A free MCP server for AI assistants at https://taxonomy.coppica.com/mcp

## About llms.txt Implementation

The Persuasion Taxonomy provides an `llms.txt` index of its catalog, foundation essays and reference pages, and an `llms-full.txt` with the full text of every technique definition.
