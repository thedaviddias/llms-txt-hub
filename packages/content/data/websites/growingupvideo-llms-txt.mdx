# GrowingUpVideo

GrowingUpVideo turns 2 to 24 photos of a person, couple or pet across the years into an AI growing\-up morph video, for birthdays, weddings and memorials\.

## Key Focus Areas

- **Photo-to-video morphing**: AI orders photos by age and renders a smooth transition between each pair.
- **People, couples and pets**: separate modes, including face matching for couples.
- **Occasion videos**: milestone birthdays, bar and bat mitzvahs, weddings, memorial tributes.
