# The SEO Agent

The SEO Agent is an automated technical SEO and AI search visibility platform built for founders and SaaS teams. It runs technical site audits, plans a 30-day keyword calendar, and publishes researched articles directly to WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Framer, or custom webhooks. Pricing starts with a US$1 3-day trial for your first 3 articles, then US$99/month for 30 articles.

## Key Focus Areas

- **Technical SEO Audits**: Automated crawl, metadata, schema, and page-level SEO issue reporting.
- **Keyword Planning**: Monthly keyword roadmaps built around product fit and search demand.
- **CMS Publishing**: Direct publishing integrations for WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Framer, and webhooks.
