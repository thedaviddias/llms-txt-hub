# CareClinic: Wellness & Symptom Tracker

CareClinic is a personal health record and journal for recording symptoms, medications, mood, pain, activity, nutrition, sleep, and vitals. Users control what they record and whether to share reports with a caregiver or health professional.

## Key Focus Areas

- Symptom and medication tracking
- Mood, sleep, nutrition, activity, and vital logs
- Personal health reports for care conversations
- iOS, Android, web, and Mac access
