## About llms.txt Implementation

One-V provides an `llms.txt` file that describes its services, case studies (NICE, Checkmarx, Quantum Machines) and open-source work. One-V also maintains [One-V LLM Serve](https://wordpress.org/plugins/one-v-llm-serve/), a free WordPress plugin that generates `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt` and serves a Markdown copy of every page for AI agents.
