# The Digicam

The Digicam is a buyer-facing reference for vintage digital and compact cameras (about 2,500 models). Each camera has a page with practical specs, battery and memory card notes, and real sample photos, so people can see what a camera shoots before buying one used.

- [Camera models](https://thedigicam.com/camera-models.html)
- [Sample photo wall](https://thedigicam.com/sample-photos.html)
- [Guides](https://thedigicam.com/articles.html)

Specs and guides are free; full sample photo sets for each camera need a small paid pass.
