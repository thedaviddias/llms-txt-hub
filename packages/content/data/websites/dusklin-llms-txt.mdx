# Dusklin

Dusklin is an AI phone receptionist for small businesses outside the United States and Canada, with pages in English, French, Spanish, Swedish, Polish and Dutch. It answers after hours, says it is an AI, takes the message, books a time the calendar has open, and sends the owner the name, number and request.

## Key Focus Areas

- An AI receptionist that answers a business line after closing
- A page and prices in local currency for each country it is open in
- Meeting notes

## About llms.txt Implementation

The llms.txt at https://dusklin.com/llms.txt lists the country pages with their prices, and https://dusklin.com/llms-full.txt carries facts for assistants: what Dusklin is and how a call goes.
