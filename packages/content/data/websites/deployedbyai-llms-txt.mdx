# deployedbyai

What actually got deployed with AI this week. Every item sourced to the publisher's own page.

## Key Focus Areas

- **The Deploy Log**: every deployment the publication has recorded, each row with its source
- **Week by week**: what shipped in AI each week, grouped by lane
- **Models**: each AI model named by rows from 2 or more companies, with every row that names it
- **MCP server**: the record for an agent, read only, over Streamable HTTP (POST); tools recent_deployments, deployments_in_week and search_deployments, every row with its source
- **Data, CSV and JSON**: the whole record, every row with its source, free to reuse under CC BY 4.0 with credit to deployedbyai
