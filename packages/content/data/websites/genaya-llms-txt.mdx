# Genaya

Genaya is one app and one AI that run a service business: an AI receptionist for calls, texts and chats, a call center, scheduling, CRM, estimates and invoices, payments and customer financing, email and SMS campaigns, online booking and forms, payroll and commissions, e-sign and video rooms. It serves field services, clinics, financial advisors, agencies and construction on web, iOS and Android.

## Key Focus Areas

- AI Calls Receptionist and AI Chat Receptionist
- Scheduling, CRM, Estimates and Invoices
- Payments and Customer Financing
- Email and SMS Campaigns
