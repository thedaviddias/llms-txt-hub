# Influence

Influence brings social campaigns, saved brand context and connected accounts into one workspace. Use Influence IQ to develop editable ideas and drafts, review the content, then schedule or publish approved posts and check delivery status.

## Key Focus Areas

- Brand-guided content and AI-assisted social planning
- Draft review, publishing and scheduling
- Available account and post Insights
- Connections with compatible AI assistants through authenticated MCP

## Public Customer Guides

- [How Influence works](https://influence.so/how-it-works): Planning, review and publishing workflow.
- [Influence IQ](https://influence.so/influence-iq): AI planning and image-generation features.
- [Publish from ChatGPT](https://influence.so/publish-from-chatgpt): Assistant connection and publishing guide.
- [Public MCP connector](https://github.com/influence-so/influence-mcp): Connection configuration and customer instructions.

## About llms.txt Implementation

Influence's [llms.txt](https://influence.so/llms.txt) links to product guidance, assistant instructions, connector documentation and service policies. Assistant connections require an Influence account, workspace permissions and OAuth or a scoped bearer key. Review and approval stay part of the publishing workflow.
