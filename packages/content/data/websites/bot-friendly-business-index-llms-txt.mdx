# Bot-Friendly Business Index

> Businesses for sale that AI agents can actually read — structured facts, stated cash-flow basis, relay-only contact

## Key Focus Areas

- Businesses for sale
- Agent-readable listings

## About llms.txt Implementation

The Bot-Friendly Business Index provides an `llms.txt` file and a full-text `llms-full.txt` covering its Agent-Readiness Standard, every listing, its buyer profiles and its broker submission route, plus structured data (listings.json with one record per listing, per-listing JSON, agents.json). The site is published in two equivalent forms — human pages and machine-readable files — with the same facts in both.
