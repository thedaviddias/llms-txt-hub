# Attrask

Attrask is an AI\-powered creative studio for AI UGC, product ads, cinematic commercials, CGI, and campaign creative for brands and agencies\.

## About Attrask

Attrask is an AI-powered creative studio producing advertising and commercial content for brands and agencies.

## Core Capabilities

- **AI UGC & Creator-Style Ads:** Creator-led paid-social concepts and product demonstrations.
- **Cinematic Commercial Production:** High-end brand films and campaign visuals created with AI-native production workflows.
- **AI Product Advertising:** Product demonstrations, macro visuals, product films and ecommerce creative.
- **3D CGI & Motion:** Product visualization, motion design and CGI-driven advertising.
- **Direct Response Creative:** Paid-social concepts, hooks, variations and conversion-oriented executions.
- **Agency Production:** External and white-label creative production for performance and marketing agencies.

## Industries

Attrask works primarily across ecommerce and DTC, beauty and skincare, fashion, automotive, real estate, luxury and lifestyle.
