# Ania

Ania makes animated characters that move their mouth with your voice in real time, on your own device. You describe a character or upload a photo, pick a style and the states (talking, greeting, thinking, yes/no, celebration) and see the price before paying. Free avatars are in the marketplace.

## Key Focus Areas

- Talking avatars for AI assistants (Ania Player), websites (React component) and videos
- Remote MCP server so Claude, ChatGPT and other assistants can create avatars and narrated videos
- Custom wake words and a creator marketplace
- Prepaid credits with the price shown before every spend
