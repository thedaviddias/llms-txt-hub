# ArbTide

Real\-time crypto funding rates and arbitrage scanner across 36 CEX and perp DEX exchanges\.

ArbTide collects real-time crypto prices, perpetual funding rates, and arbitrage spreads directly from 36 centralized and decentralized exchanges (Binance, Bybit, OKX, Hyperliquid, dYdX, etc.).

All funding rates are normalized to 8-hour intervals and annualized (APR) for accurate cross-venue comparison. Arbitrage routes and cash-and-carry spreads are calculated net of 4x trading fees and order-book slippage.

Designed for both human traders and AI agents, ArbTide exposes clean markdown feeds for every live page (via .md URL suffix) and provides a comprehensive llms.txt index covering live spreads, heatmaps, and historical data.
