# Transformer.gg

Transformer.gg is a marketplace for sponsored posts on X. A brand pastes its website and gets a list of X creators who fit what it sells, each with a suggested price, then sends offers in a few clicks. The card is charged only when a creator accepts, and the money is held until the post is live with X's Paid partnership label. Creators keep 80%.

## Key Focus Areas

- Creator discovery and suggested prices for sponsored posts on X
- Offers, payment and post tracking with tracked links
- MCP server, CLI and REST API for AI agents
- Free check of the value of one sponsored post on any X account

## About llms.txt Implementation

Transformer.gg's llms.txt explains what the marketplace does for brands and creators, how pricing works, and how agents connect through the MCP server at https://transformer.gg/mcp, the CLI and the REST API. The llms-full.txt carries the full text of the docs.
