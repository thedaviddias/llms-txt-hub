# AI Order Entry

Browser workflow that parses emailed and PDF purchase orders into structured SKU line items and ERP-ready CSV drafts for wholesale distributors.

## Key Focus Areas

- **PO Extraction**: Reads messy emailed and PDF purchase orders into normalized SKU, quantity, and pricing tables.
- **ERP-Ready Export**: Produces clean draft sales orders and CSV outputs without manual re-keying.
