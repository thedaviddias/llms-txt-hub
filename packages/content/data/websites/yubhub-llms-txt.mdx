# YubHub

YubHub reads job listings directly from employers' own careers pages and enriches them with salary, skills, work arrangement, category and industry. It exposes a free no-key JSON/XML API, free job feeds (RSS/JSON/XML), public hiring-statistics endpoints, an OpenAPI 3.0 spec, an MCP server and llms.txt. Source: https://yubhub.co
