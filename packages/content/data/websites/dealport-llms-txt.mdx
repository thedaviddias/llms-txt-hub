# Dealport

Dealport is the SMB knowledge layer for AI agents and humans, indexing cited Records across 28M+ US private companies, the buildings they own, and the human principals behind them from primary public filings.

Developers and deal teams connect through the remote Streamable HTTP MCP server, zero-dependency CLI, TypeScript SDK, Agent Skills, or REST API. Every API Pro account starts with a $5.00 free balance on a pay-as-you-go basis, while Enterprise teams talk with Dealport to run origination end-to-end.

## Key Focus Areas

- Free market sizing and buy-box filtering across 28M+ US private companies and buildings
- Cited company and property Records backed by corporate registries, UCC filings, licenses, and deeds
- Verified human owner and principal contact lookup with workspace-scoped corrections
- Living Projects and reusable Playbooks for scoring, monitoring, and pipeline tracking
- Double-opt-in owner introductions through Avery, Dealport's AI outreach specialist who always discloses she is an AI
