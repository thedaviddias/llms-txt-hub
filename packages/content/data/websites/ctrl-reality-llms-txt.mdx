# CTRL Reality

CTRL Reality, Inc. (ctrlreality.ai) records what ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok say when a buyer asks for the best business in a category. It scores those answers on AIVI with AIRS, two indices defined in Mohanty (2026), and re-runs the same prompts every month so any change is on record. Not affiliated with CTRL Reality Oy of Finland.
