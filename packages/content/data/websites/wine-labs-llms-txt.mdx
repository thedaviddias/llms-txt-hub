# Wine Labs

Wine Labs provides fine-wine market intelligence through its web application, REST API, and hosted Model Context Protocol server. Its data covers wine identity matching, merchant prices, price history, auction lots and results, critic context, and cellar workflows.

## Key Focus Areas

- Wine and LWIN identity matching
- Merchant prices, price history, and auction research
- Wine market coverage and merchant comparisons
- Account-scoped cellar and portfolio workflows
- Hosted MCP connection with browser-based OAuth sign-in
