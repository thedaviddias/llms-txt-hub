# PLNmedic

PLNmedic is a free, satirical 3D platformer browser game in which you play as a Polish doctor. You collect money, sign contracts with the National Health Fund (NFZ) and private clinics, fend off demanding patients and bureaucrats, and buy apartments across Warsaw, Sopot, Zakopane and Manhattan. It is a lighthearted satire of the Polish healthcare system.

## Key Focus Areas

- Free browser game, no install, no account, no microtransactions
- 3D platformer built with Three.js
- Satire of the Polish healthcare system
- Polish language
