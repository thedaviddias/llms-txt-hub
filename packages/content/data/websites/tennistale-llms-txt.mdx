# TennisTale

Live tennis scores, results and the full history of the sport, in one clean place\. ATP and WTA, ad\-free\.

## What TennisTale covers

- **Live scores**: every ATP and WTA singles match in play, with set scores, current game points and the server.
- **Draws and results**: full tournament draws, entry lists and results, updated as matches finish.
- **History**: every singles match of the Open era since 1968, with player profiles, career records and head-to-heads.
- **Rankings**: the current ATP and WTA rankings with weekly movement.
- **Match reports**: short written reports on tournament finals.
