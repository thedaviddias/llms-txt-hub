# Biolabmix

Manufacturer of reagents, kits, and components for molecular biology, genetics, diagnostics, and biotechnology. The catalog includes PCR mixes for real-time and classical PCR, DNA and RNA extraction kits, enzymes, components and kits for in vitro mRNA synthesis, oligonucleotides, pathogen detection test systems (SPF vivariums, poultry farming, aquaculture, agriculture), and equipment for PCR laboratories.
