# FabulAnna

FabulAnna is a web app where a child aged 3 to 12 invents a story aloud with a grown-up next to them. The child picks what happens on each page, their favourite soft toy becomes the main character and their own drawings turn into the illustrations. In about half an hour the family has an illustrated story or comic, which can also be ordered as a printed book. The first story is free. Available in Spanish and Catalan.

## Key Focus Areas

- Storytelling with children aged 3 to 12
- Children's own drawings as book illustrations
- Printed picture books and comics
- Dialogic reading for families

## About llms.txt Implementation

FabulAnna's llms.txt is written in Spanish. It summarises what the product is, lists current prices, and links every public page with a one-line description: how it works, the example stories, the guides for parents on inventing a story or a comic by hand, safety and privacy.
