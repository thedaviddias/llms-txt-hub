# AAA AI

AAA AI (Autonomous Agents Automations) is an enterprise multi-agent AI platform. Orchestrate expert models, tools, memory, and human approvals. Includes Meet for voice and video calls with live AI notes.

## Key Focus Areas

- Multi-agent orchestration
- AI meeting notes (Meet)
- Agent discovery and payments
- SaaS and self-host

## About llms.txt Implementation

AAA AI publishes llms.txt, llms-full.txt, ai.txt, agents.md, and machine-readable agent discovery under `/.well-known/` (agent.json, agent-payments.json, MCP server card, agent skills).
