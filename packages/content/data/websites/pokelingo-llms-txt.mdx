# Pokelingo

Pokelingo is a Pokédex in nine languages: Japanese, English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Korean, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. Every Pokémon, move and ability is listed under its official name in each language, with the official Pokédex entries side by side. It also has a daily riddle (read one Pokédex entry, name the Pokémon), a type chart with a matchup calculator, and quizzes.

## Key Focus Areas

- Pokémon names in nine languages
- Official Pokédex entries by game
- Type chart and matchup calculator
- Daily Pokédex riddle and quizzes
