# VoxScreen

One\-way video interviews for small hiring teams\. AI scores each answer against your rubric and shows why\. Permanent free plan, 50 candidates a month\.

## What VoxScreen does

VoxScreen is a small, independent software company making one-way video interview software for small hiring teams, founders and independent recruiters. Candidates record their answers on a phone or laptop, with no app and no account. Each answer is transcribed and scored against the employer's rubric, criterion by criterion, with the reasoning shown next to the transcript.
