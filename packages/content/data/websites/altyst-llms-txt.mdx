# Altyst

Altyst is commercial real estate underwriting software. It reads the documents a deal already comes with, an offering memorandum, a rent roll, a T-12, a debt quote, and produces an editable model plus the Excel workbook and PDF documents an investment committee expects. A large part of the surface is free and open to anyone without an account: ten calculators at https://altyst.ai/tools, embeddable versions of the same calculators at https://altyst.ai/embed, a ten-year multifamily acquisition model with 2,718 live formulas across eleven tabs at https://altyst.ai/tools/multifamily-model, and a 60-term glossary at https://altyst.ai/glossary. The financial results are computed by a deterministic engine in exact decimal arithmetic; AI is used only to read documents and propose values that a human reviews, never to perform the calculation.

## Key Focus Areas

- Free calculators, ten of them, covering NOI, loan sizing, cap rate, DSCR, cash-on-cash, gross rent multiplier, break-even occupancy, debt yield, commercial mortgage and IRR, each stating the lender or market convention its answer follows
- Embeddable calculators any site may iframe, with the credit line as the entire licence and no advertising inside the frame
- Free multifamily acquisition model in Excel, eleven tabs, nothing locked and no macros
- Glossary of 60 underwriting terms, each with the formula, a worked numeric example and the common mistake
- Document extraction into a live model, where every input traces to the document it was read from and every output carries the formula that produced it

## About llms.txt Implementation

Altyst publishes llms.txt as a routed index of the product surface and llms-full.txt as the expanded reference. Both enumerate each free calculator with the exact quantity it solves for, the glossary definitions with their formulas, and the plain-language answers at https://altyst.ai/answers, so an assistant asked how to compute debt yield, size a loan against three lender tests, or reconcile economic and physical occupancy can cite a specific page rather than paraphrase. They also state plainly which numbers come from the deterministic engine and which values were proposed by document extraction.
