# Casama

AI teammates that do real work on computers of their own, remember how you like things and ask before anything risky\. Free personal self\-host or Cloud\.

## What is Casama?

Casama gives people AI teammates, called bots, that do real work on computers of their own: they browse, fill in forms, write files and run code on a sandboxed Linux machine you can watch live and take over. Bots remember what matters, run on schedules and triggers, and pause for your approval before risky steps such as sending, paying or deleting.

Casama's apps, `computerd`, SDKs, CLI, MCP server, safety rules, bot computer image and egress proxy are open source (AGPL-3.0 and Apache-2.0); the Casama Engine is closed. Run it on Casama Cloud (free to start) or self-host with a free personal license.
