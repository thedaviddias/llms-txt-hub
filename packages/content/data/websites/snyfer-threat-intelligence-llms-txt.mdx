# Snyfer Threat Intelligence

Look beyond a website with Snyfer\. Run a scan, inspect forensic evidence, explore connected domains and understand the findings behind its Trust Score\.

## About Snyfer

Snyfer is a website security and threat intelligence platform built around its own website scanning and forensic analysis engine.

The Snyfer scanner directly analyzes websites and their technical environment, collecting first-party observations from rendered pages, network activity, redirects, HTTP responses, technologies, DNS, TLS, hosting infrastructure, scripts, assets and technical fingerprints.

These first-party findings are enriched with selected external intelligence sources, including antivirus detections, reputation databases, blacklists and financial regulator warnings.

Snyfer then correlates these signals to identify technical relationships between websites, related infrastructure and potential threat networks.

## Core Capabilities

- **Proprietary Website Scanner**: Snyfer's own scanning engine analyzes websites and collects technical evidence directly.
- **Website Forensics**: Inspect network requests, redirects, headers, scripts, technologies, assets and page behavior.
- **Infrastructure Analysis**: Analyze DNS, IP, ASN, hosting, TLS certificates and domain registration data.
- **Technical Fingerprinting**: Detect shared scripts, assets, favicons, tracking identifiers and other technical fingerprints.
- **Threat Correlation**: Connect domains through shared infrastructure and technical indicators.
- **Threat Networks**: Identify and investigate clusters of technically related websites.
- **Trust Score**: Combine observed evidence and external intelligence into a structured risk assessment.
- **Regulatory Intelligence**: Surface relevant warnings published by financial regulators.
- **Reputation Intelligence**: Enrich Snyfer observations with antivirus, blacklist and reputation signals.
- **Historical Observations**: Preserve previously observed website and infrastructure information.

## How Snyfer Data Is Produced

Snyfer is not simply an aggregation interface for third-party APIs.

The core dataset originates from Snyfer's own scanning and analysis infrastructure. External intelligence is used as an enrichment layer alongside evidence collected directly by the Snyfer scanner.

This combination allows Snyfer reports to present both independently observed technical evidence and relevant third-party intelligence in a single investigation.
