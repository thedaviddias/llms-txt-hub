# Myoils.club

Myoils.club is an independent guide run by a dōTERRA Wellness Advocate. It explains customer enrollment, market-specific fees, starter kits and essential-oil prices, and includes a free calculator for comparing retail purchases with a wholesale customer account. It is not an official dōTERRA website.

The llms.txt file links to country guides, oil information, calculator pages and privacy information. It distinguishes markets from interface languages and states important scope limits, including that Mainland China enrollment is not supported. It does not provide medical advice or promise income.
