# obrotomierz

obrotomierz is a Polish service for savers. It tracks bank promotions (account opening bonuses, deposits, savings accounts and business accounts), reads their conditions and deadlines from the banks' own terms, and keeps two separate timelines for each offer: the conditions for getting the bonus and the conditions for keeping the account free of charge. It covers only deposit products guaranteed up to 100,000 euro and does not give investment advice. A companion app for iOS and Android uses the same catalog.

## Key Focus Areas

- Bank promotions and account opening bonuses in Poland
- Deposits, savings accounts and treasury savings bonds
- Answers on how promotion conditions work (transfers, card payments, new customer rules)
- Original analyses based on an archive of about 2,670 promotion terms

## About llms.txt Implementation

The llms.txt (in Polish) lists the answer pages on how promotion conditions work and the original analyses, each with a one-paragraph summary and its data source. The llms-full.txt contains the whole offer catalog in tables, the answers, the conclusions of the analyses and the transfer session times of 19 Polish banks in a single file.
