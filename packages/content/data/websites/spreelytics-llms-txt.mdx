# Spreelytics

Switch from GA4 to analytics that shows the revenue each channel brings in\. Free up to 100k events a month\.

## What Spreelytics does

Spreelytics is web and product analytics that shows where your revenue comes from. One script tag records traffic, sources and pages, and payments from Stripe, Paddle, Polar or Lemon Squeezy are credited to the channel that earned them.

## Key features

- **Revenue attribution:** MRR, ARR and net revenue by channel, page and campaign, last-touch or linear.
- **Product analytics:** funnels, journeys, retention, goals and a timeline for each person.
- **AI traffic:** visits from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and Gemini get their own channel, and AI crawlers are counted server-side.
- **Search Console:** queries, clicks and positions next to your traffic.
- **MCP server:** Claude Code, Cursor, Windsurf or VS Code can read your data and build goals, funnels and dashboards, on every plan.
