# tudlora

Browse courses from across the major learning platforms, grouped into guided paths so you can compare your options and find the right place to start\.

## Key Focus Areas

- **Career-First Discovery**: Courses are organized around professions, not subjects. Pick the career you're moving toward, and Tudlora shows the skills it takes and the courses that teach them.
- **Skills Mapping**: Each career breaks down into the specific skills employers look for, with each skill linked to a curated set of courses.
- **Course Comparison**: Put courses side by side and compare them on what matters before you commit your time and money.
- **Support for AI-Displaced Workers**: Built for people whose jobs are being reshaped by AI and who need a clear, practical route into their next role.

## About llms.txt Implementation

Tudlora's llms.txt gives language models a map of how the site is organized: careers at /careers/, individual skills at /skills/, and focused learning guides at /learn/. This helps AI assistants point people to the right career path, skill page, or course list when they ask how to retrain for a new role.

## Features

- Career paths: Browse professions and see the skills each one requires, laid out in one place.
- Side-by-side comparison: Compare courses from different providers against each other instead of juggling open tabs.
- Multi-provider catalog: Courses from Coursera, Pluralsight, DataCamp, boot.dev, and more, gathered under one roof.
- Learning guides: Focused pages like "Best JavaScript Courses for Beginners" that answer a specific question quickly.

## Getting Started

Go to tudlora.com and start with the career you want to move into. From there, see the skills that role needs, open any skill to find courses that teach it, and compare your options side by side before choosing where to learn.
