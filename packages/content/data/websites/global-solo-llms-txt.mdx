# Global Solo

Global Solo compares US LLC formation, banking, tax and compliance services for founders who are not US tax residents. It is affiliate-funded and publishes its selection method. It also maintains the Banking Access Index, a CC BY 4.0 dataset covering 19 US business banking providers and owners from 8 countries of residence. Each cell records whether the provider accepts in writing, publishes a restriction, publishes no restriction, or publishes no policy at all, with a quote from the provider's own page. Silence is never counted as acceptance.

## Key Focus Areas

- US LLC formation and registered agents for non-residents
- US business banking access by country of residence
- Form 5472 and other information-return penalties
- Country guides for founders in India, Pakistan, Nigeria and five other countries

## About llms.txt Implementation

The llms.txt lists the site's guides, comparisons, country pages, calculators and datasets by section, with a one-line summary per link. The llms-full.txt carries the underlying data as tables: formation pricing, US banking for non-residents, the Banking Access Index matrix (regenerated from the published dataset) and Form 5472 penalties.
