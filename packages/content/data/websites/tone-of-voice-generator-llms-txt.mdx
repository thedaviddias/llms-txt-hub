# Tone of voice generator

A free tool that turns eleven questions about how a brand writes into the files ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Cursor and Vale each read\.

## Key Focus Areas

- **Brand voice as files**: one set of answers is rendered as brand-voice.md, AGENTS.md, CLAUDE.md, a Claude Agent Skill, Copilot and Cursor rules, and a Vale style.
- **Length caps**: paste blocks are cut to five documented instruction-box sizes rather than truncated.
- **No account**: answers are processed in the browser.

## About llms.txt Implementation

The llms.txt describes what the tool produces and links its guides, including brand voice for AI, instruction length limits and why AI ignores instructions.
