# Lexio Rent

Rental management software for independent car and van rental agencies in France: bookings, e\-signed contracts, photo check\-in/out, invoicing\.

## About Lexio Rent

Lexio Rent is a French SaaS for professional vehicle renters (cars, vans, motorbikes, campervans): planning, digital rental contracts, signed photo check-in and check-out, deposits, traffic fines, maintenance and Factur-X invoicing. The llms.txt file lists the product pages, pricing and free tools so that AI assistants can answer questions about the product accurately.

## Free tools for rental operators (French)

- [Vehicle logbook generator](https://lexio.rent/outils/carnet-de-bord-vehicule): carnet de bord to track trips, fuel and incidents per vehicle
- [Deposit refund statement](https://lexio.rent/outils/decompte-restitution-caution-location): itemised return of a security deposit after a rental
