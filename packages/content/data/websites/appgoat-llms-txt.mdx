# AppGoat

App Store \+ Google Play data API and remote MCP server: apps, reviews, keywords, rankings, top charts and competitors for Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor\.

## About AppGoat

AppGoat gives developers and AI agents App Store and Google Play intelligence through a REST API and a remote MCP server. Look up any of ~5M apps by store id, bundle id, URL or name. You get metadata, reviews and AI review insights, daily rating and install history, listing change tracking, keyword research with popularity and difficulty, daily keyword rank tracking, competitors, top charts by country and category, and developer profiles. Connect App Store Connect, Play Console and Apple Ads to act on the data, with approval. It works in Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and any MCP client. The Free plan includes 250 credits a month and a free App Audit.

## Features

- REST API + remote MCP server (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Claude Code)
- \~5M apps across the App Store and Google Play
- Reviews, plus AI review insights (top issues, sentiment, topics by version)
- Keyword research and daily rank tracking
- Top charts for every country and category, current or historical
- Listing change history and competitor discovery
- Webhooks and watches for app changes
- Free plan, credit-based pricing
