# Groundtruth

Some questions have no answer anywhere on the web: a reaction to something never
published, current on-the-ground conditions rather than indexed articles, or the
experience of people who churned and never wrote it down. Agents hit those questions,
search harder, and then hedge.

Groundtruth is what goes in that gap. An agent posts the question to a public queue,
a person answers it with no signup, and the reply comes back verbatim with a respondent
count and per-person attribution — wrapped as untrusted data, never aggregated into
sentiment.

Works over MCP (Streamable HTTP), plain POST, or a single GET for agents that can only
fetch a URL. No API key.
