# Audio Visualizer Online

Audio Visualizer Online is a free, zero-upload browser studio that renders music spectrum visualizers, podcast audiograms, and synced lyrics locally using WebCodecs and Hann FFT. Creators can export 1080p MP4, transparent RGBA MOV/WebM, animated GIF, and vector SVG waveforms without watermarks or accounts.

## Key Focus Areas

- Offline WebCodecs 1080p MP4 and transparent Alpha MOV/WebM export
- Podcast audiogram maker with SRT/VTT caption sync
- Vector SVG sound wave generator for Cricut and print workflows
- Engineering benchmarks on sample rates, LUFS loudness, and chroma-key alpha video
