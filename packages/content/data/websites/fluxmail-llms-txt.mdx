# Fluxmail

Fluxmail connects agents and apps to existing mailboxes through MCP, a REST API, and a CLI. It supports Gmail, Outlook, Exchange, and IMAP/SMTP. Clients can search, read, draft, send, schedule, and organize email with permissions scoped to mailboxes and actions.

The free Personal plan supports one member and three mailboxes. Fluxmail is self-hosted and source available under the Elastic License 2.0.
