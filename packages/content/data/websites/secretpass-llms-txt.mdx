# SecretPass

SecretPass is a shopping club with coupon codes and deals for online shopping stores in the US, UK and Australia, with an open MCP server for AI assistants\.

SecretPass is a shopping club with coupon codes and deals for online shopping stores in the United States, the United Kingdom and Australia. Its open Model Context Protocol (MCP) server lets AI assistants, agents and shoppers search current vouchers, coupons and discounts.

## MCP server

- URL: `https://secretpass.com/mcp` (Streamable HTTP, no authentication)
- Tools: `search_offers`, `get_offer`, `list_filters`
- Docs: [secretpass.com/mcp-api-docs](https://secretpass.com/mcp-api-docs)

Search needs no account or API key. Voucher codes are for members and are not included in public results.
