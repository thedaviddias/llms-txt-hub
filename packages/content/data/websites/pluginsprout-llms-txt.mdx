# PluginSprout

PluginSprout makes WordPress plugins that each do one job. Most are add-ons that fill gaps in LearnDash, AffiliateWP and WooCommerce. Two standalone plugins cover appointment booking (Appointure) and malware scanning (Security Guard).

## Key Focus Areas

- LearnDash add-ons
- AffiliateWP add-ons
- WooCommerce add-ons
- WordPress plugins
