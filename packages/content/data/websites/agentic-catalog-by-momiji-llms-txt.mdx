# Agentic Catalog by momiji

A Shopify app that checks robots.txt and llms.txt rules for GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and PerplexityBot, audits Product JSON-LD and Open Graph completeness on live product pages, and reviews policy, shipping, tax, domain, and product data in one readiness score.

## Key Focus Areas

- robots.txt and llms.txt rules for GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and PerplexityBot
- Product JSON-LD and Open Graph completeness on live product pages
- Policy, shipping, tax, domain, and product data reviewed in one readiness score

The developer site publishes an llms.txt at https://apify.momiji-space.com/llms.txt that indexes its Apify Actors and Shopify apps, including Agentic Catalog by momiji (https://apps.shopify.com/agentic-catalog-readiness).
