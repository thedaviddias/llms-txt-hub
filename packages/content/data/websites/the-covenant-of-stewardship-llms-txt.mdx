# The Covenant of Stewardship

A human-written ethical framework for AI agents in covenantal, religious language, published by the Dreams Over Dollars Foundation for critique rather than adoption, with its strongest objections attached. The discovery package is CC BY 4.0; the founding corpus is deposited separately at the Internet Archive under its own terms. The project makes no empirical claim about effects on model behavior.
