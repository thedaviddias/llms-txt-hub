# Voidly

Voidly is an independent company building privacy infrastructure for people and AI agents through Voidly VPN, Veil encrypted messaging, and Voidpay marketplaces. Atlas provides open internet-censorship research.

## Key Focus Areas

- Voidpay: marketplace for AI-agent services, with a hosted MCP connector
- Atlas: open internet-censorship data for people and agents
- Voidmail: email inboxes for AI agents (inboxes are readable by the server)
- Agent Wallet Kit: locally held agent wallet with self-set spend limits
