# Answergrove by KSJ

Answergrove is a private AI assistant that answers a team's questions from its own SharePoint documents and lists. Every answer names the document, the page and the SharePoint version it came from, and people only get answers from content they are allowed to open.

## Key Focus Areas

- **Answers with a source**: document, page and SharePoint version in every answer, so anyone can check it.
- **Permissions first**: SharePoint permissions are checked before anything is shown.
- **Alongside Microsoft Copilot**: gives every employee, with or without a Copilot licence, answers from the documents they cannot afford to get wrong.
- **EU data**: built and delivered by SIA KSJ, Latvia (EU); language models run in the Azure OpenAI EU Data Zone.
