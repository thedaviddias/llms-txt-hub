# Forever In Our Hearts

Forever In Our Hearts lets families create an online memorial page for a person or pet, with the life story, photos, service details and tributes from family and friends, shared by link or QR code. Funeral homes can publish memorials and online funeral notices under their own brand.

## Key Focus Areas

- Online memorial websites for people and pets
- Tributes, photo galleries and timelines
- QR code memorials for headstones, plaques and funeral programs
- Funeral notices and the Funeral Director Network
- Public read-only MCP server for AI assistants (https://mcp.foreverinourhearts.co/mcp)

## About llms.txt Implementation

The llms.txt summarises the service, prices by country, the main pages and the MCP server, so AI assistants can answer questions about creating an online memorial and link to the right page.
