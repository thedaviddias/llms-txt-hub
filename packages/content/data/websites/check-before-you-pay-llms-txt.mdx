# Check before you pay

Check before you pay shows what happened to the entries that paid the registration fee of a Bittensor subnet, measured from the chain and refreshed every day: how many were still earning after one and two weeks, how many got their fee back, what a new entry is paid a day today and how long today's fee takes to come back. Every page also exists as plain text and as data for agents, and the same reads are offered as three tools through an MCP server at https://check.loopholetape.com/mcp.
