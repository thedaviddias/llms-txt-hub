# TestMu AI (Formerly LambdaTest)

TestMu AI (Formerly LambdaTest) is a full stack agentic AI quality engineering platform. Its AI agents plan, author, execute and analyze end-to-end tests, and teams use it to test web, mobile and enterprise applications across real devices and real browsers.
