# Insurance Panda

Free Web/iOS/Android car insurance comparison \& education app: 5\-digit ZIP quotes, local renewal reminders \& coverage guides—no account\. Not an insurer\.

## Key Focus Areas

- **Car Insurance Quote Comparison**: Start comparing auto insurance quotes with a 5-digit ZIP code—free on Web, iOS, and Android, with no account required.
- **Plain-English Coverage Guides**: Six practical guides covering comparison shopping, full coverage vs. liability, state minimum requirements, tickets and accidents, teen drivers, and high-risk drivers.
- **Insurer Reviews & Driver FAQs**: Independent reviews of major auto insurers (such as GEICO, Erie, and Farmers) alongside answers to everyday claims, discount, and policy questions.
- **Local-Only Renewal Reminders**: Custom policy renewal and shopping reminders with calendar and AM/PM controls—both remembered ZIP codes and reminders stay locally on the user's device.
