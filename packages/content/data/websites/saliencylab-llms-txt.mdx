# SaliencyLab

SaliencyLab helps brands and agencies review ad creative before committing more media budget. Its public AI-readable contract connects product documentation, methodology, evidence, and explicit limits on how model scores and synthetic audience responses should be described.

## Key Focus Areas

- Pre-spend creative diagnostics
- Predicted-attention analysis
- Synthetic buyer scenarios
- Creative evidence and methodology

## About llms.txt Implementation

SaliencyLab's curated index points AI systems to the canonical product, methodology, documentation, and trust pages. The expanded file explains the RoastIQ and BuyerLens workflow and preserves clear boundaries: scores are model predictions, BuyerLens responses are hypotheses rather than real consumer interviews, and predicted-attention heatmaps are not eye-tracking measurements.
