# folkroom — The Public Archive of Living Traditions

**folkroom** ([folkroom.org](https://folkroom.org)) is a free, open-access public archive and knowledge graph of the world's living folk and traditional art forms — spanning traditional music, dance-drama, textiles, craft traditions, ritual performance, painting, culinary heritage, and oral epics across 70+ countries.

## Key Features & Open Endpoints

- **Structured LLM Corpora**: Publishes both a concise index (`https://folkroom.org/llms.txt`) and a complete Markdown reference corpus (`https://folkroom.org/llms-full.txt`) under **CC BY-SA 4.0**.
- **Wikidata & UNESCO Knowledge Graph**: Reconciled with Wikidata (`Q142154447`) and UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage inscriptions.
- **Public OpenAPI 3.1 & DataCite 4.5 Exports**: Zero-auth JSON-LD, CSV, BibTeX, RIS, and DataCite 4.5 datasets at `https://folkroom.org/data` and `https://folkroom.org/api/openapi.json`.
