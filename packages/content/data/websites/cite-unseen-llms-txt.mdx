# Cite Unseen

Cite Unseen audits the conditions a website controls that affect whether AI answer engines can understand, trust and cite the organization behind it. The free scan needs no account: it reads a site's robots.txt, sitemap and up to eight pages as raw HTML and runs thirteen deterministic checks in about twenty seconds. Paid audits rate all 33 checks in a published rubric, with a person signing each rating that needs judgment.

## Key Focus Areas

- Crawler rules, sitemaps, canonicals and rendered text
- Structured data that agrees with the visible page
- llms.txt and other machine-readable guidance, scored for accuracy rather than presence
- Every finding linked to a captured, timestamped observation of the live site

## About llms.txt Implementation

The site's llms.txt and llms-full.txt are generated from the same source as its sitemap and pages, so the three cannot disagree. llms-full.txt carries the full text of every published page.
