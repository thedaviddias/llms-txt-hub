# flatpass

> flatpass turns Discord roles into paid subscriptions that settle in the seller's own Stripe account through Stripe Connect Standard; flatpass never holds funds. Members subscribe on a hosted invite page, sign in with Discord, pay on Stripe Checkout, and the bot assigns the role in seconds and removes it when the subscription ends. Pricing is a flat monthly plan, free up to 15 paying members, with 0% of sales on every plan.

## Key Focus Areas

- Paid Discord roles on the seller's own Stripe account
- Flat monthly pricing metered by paying members
- Instant role grant and removal, multiple tiers, trials and promo codes
- Fee comparisons with other Discord monetization platforms
