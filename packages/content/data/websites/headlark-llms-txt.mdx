# Headlark

Writes SEO metadata at build time: titles, descriptions, link previews, JSON\-LD and alt text\. Free llms\.txt generator from your sitemap too\.

Submitted by the Headlark team. Headlark writes each page's title, meta description, link preview tags, JSON-LD and alt text at build or deploy time, from the page's own content. Free for 25 pages.

We also make a free [llms.txt generator](https://headlark.com/tools/llms-txt-generator): it reads a sitemap, fetches up to 50 pages and writes an llms.txt in the llmstxt.org format. It runs on our server, needs no signup and stores nothing.
