# Tinker AI Lab

Tinker AI Lab is a bilingual (English & Simplified Chinese) reproducible AI engineering lab providing hardware-tested Local LLM benchmarks across Apple Silicon Unified Memory, NVIDIA RTX GPUs, AMD Strix Halo, and Raspberry Pi 5 edge nodes, alongside an interactive VRAM & KV Cache Calculator, parameterized Docker/n8n configs, and step-by-step API troubleshooting trees.
