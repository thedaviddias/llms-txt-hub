# AgenticCart

AgenticCart gets a WooCommerce or Shopify catalog in front of AI shopping assistants: product feeds for ChatGPT and Google, the store's own app in ChatGPT and Claude, and an AI storefront on the store's domain. Checkout stays in the store.
