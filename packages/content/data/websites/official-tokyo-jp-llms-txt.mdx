# official.tokyo.jp

official.tokyo.jp gives foreign residents of Tokyo a short outline of everyday procedures and links to the official national, metropolitan and ward pages. Each page shows the date a person last checked the official sources. It is not a government website; if anything differs from an official page, the official page is correct.

## Key Focus Areas

- Moving in: resident registration, residence card, My Number, utilities
- Health insurance, pension and resident tax
- Extension of period of stay
- Ward office data for all 23 wards of Tokyo
- Emergency and disaster information

## About llms.txt Implementation

official.tokyo.jp provides `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt`, a JSON API with an OpenAPI description, Markdown mirrors of every page, and a read-only MCP server. Data is licensed under CC BY 4.0.
