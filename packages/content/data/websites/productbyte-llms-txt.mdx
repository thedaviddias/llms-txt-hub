# ProductByte

ProductByte helps e-commerce brands post, advertise and measure from one workspace. Schedule and publish social posts to Instagram, TikTok, X, Threads, Bluesky and Pinterest, plan ad campaigns, and compare social and ad results to see what works, then adjust. Studio makes on-brand images and carousels from saved brand playbooks. Nibble, ProductByte's agent, does the work from a single sentence, and a remote MCP server gives any AI agent the same connection. It also connects your shop (Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, Google Sheets) and syncs bol.com, TikTok Shop and product feeds when you need them.

## Key Focus Areas

- Social post planning, scheduling and publishing
- Ad campaigns and ad performance
- Social and ad insights in one place
- On-brand images and carousels from brand playbooks
- Remote MCP server for AI agents
- Shop connectors, marketplaces and product feeds
