# NeverApply

NeverApply is an AI job-search agent. It matches job seekers to roles, tailors resumes, answers screening questions, prepares candidates for interviews, and applies to jobs after per-job approval, through Send mode, or with a configured assistant send grant.

## Key Focus Areas

- Hosted MCP server at https://neverapply.co/mcp (Streamable HTTP, OAuth 2.1 with PKCE)
- MCP setup documentation for Claude, Cursor, Cline and other clients
- Job matching, resume tailoring and application tracking
