# TransparentCars

Free, key\-free JSON API for the Portuguese car market Estimate ISV and IUC from engine size, CO₂, fuel and year; get market valuations from comparable listings\.

Free, key-free JSON API for the Portuguese car market. Estimate **ISV** (import tax) and **IUC** (annual road tax) from engine size, CO₂, fuel and year; get market **valuations** from comparable listings; browse used-car **stock**. No sign-up, fair-use per-IP rate limits. Tax figures are a maintained estimate, cross-checked against the official AT simulator (not the official figure).
