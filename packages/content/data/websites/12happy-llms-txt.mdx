# 12happy

12happy checks websites, APIs and cron jobs and alerts by email, Slack, Telegram or push, and by SMS and webhooks on paid plans. Every failure is confirmed from a second location before it alerts. Each plan includes a public status page, and the free plan covers 50 monitors.
