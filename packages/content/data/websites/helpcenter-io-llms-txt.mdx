# HelpCenter.io

HelpCenter.io is help center and knowledge base software for SaaS and online businesses. It hosts branded help centers on a customer subdomain, on a custom domain or, on some plans, in a subfolder of the customer's own website. It also provides FAQ sections for any website and an AI Widget that puts the help center inside a product.

## Key Focus Areas

- Hosted, branded help centers and knowledge bases
- FAQ sections embedded on other websites with one snippet
- AI Widget and Ask AI answers drawn from the customer's own published articles
- Typo-tolerant instant search, plus semantic AI search on the Catalyst plan or as an add-on
- Free, done-for-you migration from other knowledge base providers, plus self-service imports
- An MCP server and plugins for Claude and ChatGPT, and a read-only /mcp endpoint on each public help center

## About llms.txt Implementation

HelpCenter.io publishes llms.txt and llms-full.txt at the apex domain. These files describe its plans, product pages, AI features, developer documentation, comparisons and guides. Help centers built on HelpCenter.io are also agent-readable through Markdown, llms.txt and ai.txt. Without an account, an AI agent can create a private help center, migrate up to 500 articles into it, and hand its user a claim link to take ownership (see https://helpcenter.io/for-ai-agents).
