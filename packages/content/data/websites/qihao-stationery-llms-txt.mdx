# Qihao Stationery

> Zhejiang Qihao Stationery Co., Ltd. is a source manufacturer of creative erasers,
> founded in 2010 in Hengdian, Dongyang, Zhejiang. In-house processes cover tooling,
> injection moulding, extrusion and packaging, with an annual capacity of about
> 200 million pieces. Products are tested by SGS, BV and INTERTEK against EN 71,
> ASTM F963, CPSIA and 7P phthalates limits.
