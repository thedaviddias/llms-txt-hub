# Saachi Japanese Institute BLR

Japanese classes in Bangalore for JLPT N5–N2, online and in\-person\. Native\-trainer sessions, small batches, 90% JLPT success\. Book a free demo\.

## About Saachi

**Saachi Japanese Language Institute** is a Bengaluru-based Japanese language school with 18+ years of teaching experience. We prepare learners in India for the JLPT from N5 to N2 and train companies that work with Japan.

## Key Focus Areas

- **JLPT Courses (N5–N2)**: Live, instructor-led classes in small batches
- **Corporate Training**: Business Japanese and cross-cultural training for teams
- **Translation & Interpretation**: Japanese–English language services for businesses
- **JLPT Guidance for Indian Learners**: Level selection, exam dates and preparation

## Courses at a Glance

- **JLPT N5**: ~130 kanji, 100-hour course, for complete beginners
- **JLPT N4**: ~340 kanji, 100-hour course
- **JLPT N3**: ~700 kanji, 110-hour course
- **JLPT N2**: ~1,200 kanji, 130-hour course
