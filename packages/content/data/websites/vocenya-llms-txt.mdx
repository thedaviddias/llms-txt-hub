# Vocenya

Vocenya, made by GH Business Solutions, is an AI receptionist for local and small businesses. It answers phone calls and website chats 24/7, books appointments into the business calendar, captures leads, hands calls to trained human agents or the owner, and places outbound AI calls with Do Not Call, consent and calling-hours checks.

## Key Focus Areas

- AI receptionist and website live chat for small businesses
- Plans, add-ons and industry pages published as Markdown
- REST API, webhooks and MCP servers for developers
