# Explore Java Indonesia \(EJI\)

Explore Java Indonesia \(EJI\) is a private tour operator in Yogyakarta, guiding international travellers to Borobudur, Bromo, Ijen, Tumpak Sewu and across Java\.

## About Explore Java Indonesia (EJI)

Explore Java Indonesia is a private tour operator based in Yogyakarta. We guide international travellers across Java, from Borobudur and Prambanan to Mount Bromo, Ijen and Tumpak Sewu, and on to Bali. Every tour is private: your own driver-guide and vehicle, no shared groups.

## What makes EJI different

- **Private by default.** Your group only, at your pace.
- **Bromo + Ijen by train, ending in Bali.** One connected journey, with no backtracking to Yogyakarta.
- **Plan B built in.** Volcano weather, closures and crowds are planned for, not left to chance.
- **Honest about nature.** We tell you plainly what a sunrise or a crater can and cannot promise.
- **Crowd planning.** Timings chosen to avoid the busiest hours at the major temples.
- **Paperwork handled.** Tickets, permits and entry rules are sorted for you.
- **Cruise-port tours planned backwards.** From your all-aboard time, with a next-port guarantee.
- **Low-risk booking.** Clear prices, clear policies, a real person on WhatsApp.
