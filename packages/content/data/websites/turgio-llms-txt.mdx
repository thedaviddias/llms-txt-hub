# Turgio

Turgio is a web ordering system for Lithuanian wholesalers and suppliers (fruit and vegetables, meat and fish, drinks, and farmers selling to restaurants, cafes and shops). Customers order on their phone from a link, with no app to install. One daily list shows what to buy, how to split it per customer and where to deliver. Per-customer prices, stock, returnable crates and pallets, customer debts, VAT invoices, CSV export and i.SAF export (Pro plan). Lithuanian and Russian.

## Key Focus Areas

- B2B ordering for food wholesalers
- Daily buying list and delivery routes
- Returnable packaging (crates, pallets) tracking
- Customer debts and VAT invoices

## About llms.txt Implementation

Turgio provides an `llms.txt` file with AI-readable information about the product, pricing plans and guides, plus an `llms-full.txt` with every guide in full, in Lithuanian, Russian and English.
