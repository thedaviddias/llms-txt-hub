# Social Expert

Social Expert is an independent service by ForUsApps, LLC for TikTok content
research and draft-video feedback. It is not affiliated with TikTok.

## Key Focus Areas

- Trends, posting times, topic-based content ideas, known sounds, keywords and hashtags
- Uploaded draft analysis with timestamped feedback and saved reports
- Hosted Streamable HTTP MCP with OAuth and documented client setup
- Dated trend articles with TikTok links and monthly archives

## About llms.txt Implementation

The public `llms.txt` links to setup instructions, supported capabilities, original
articles, privacy and terms. It identifies the branded MCP endpoint and explains
account authorization, shared usage, limited one-time free allowances and paid
monthly plans. Tool calls can consume allowance and create saved analysis state.
Uploaded videos and reports are retained for 14 days. Social Expert does not
publish or schedule posts, edit videos or guarantee growth.
