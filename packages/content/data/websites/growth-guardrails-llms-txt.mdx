# growth-guardrails

Open-source AI skill (MIT) that turns "how did this software get on my machine without me asking?" into an actionable distribution & user-acquisition playbook with built-in compliance guardrails. Includes sourced case studies of AdBlock Plus / Acceptable Ads and Qihoo 360, a China/US/EU legal matrix at statute level, 8 compliant tactics, 10 red lines, and an 18-item self-audit checklist.
