# Adgent

Multiplayer AI for teams running Meta and Google Ads\. Aida joins your meetings with account intelligence; Adgent carries the decision into completed work\.

## What Adgent does

Adgent is a multiplayer AI for agencies and brand teams that run Meta and Google Ads.

- **In meetings:** Aida, Adgent's meeting participant, joins with the account's data, confirmed goals and past decisions in view.
- **After meetings:** Adgent carries the agreed decision into a report, a recurring agent or an account change.
- **Every day:** ten specialist agents read each connected account and rank what to fix by estimated monthly value.
