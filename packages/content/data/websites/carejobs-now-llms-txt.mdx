# CareJobs.Now

CareJobs\.Now is a free job board for everyone who works in US healthcare\. It reads the career sites of 270 hospitals, health systems, clinic groups and care employers every day and shows the pay each employer posts\.

## Key Focus Areas

- **Healthcare jobs**: open nursing, physician, NP and PA, CNA, therapy, imaging, lab, pharmacy and front-office jobs, each linking to the employer's own application.
- **Posted pay**: the hourly or yearly pay employers post, measured by kind of job, employer and state.
- **Open data for agents**: a free remote MCP server (https://carejobs.now/api/mcp) and JSON endpoints documented at https://carejobs.now/data.

## About llms.txt Implementation

The llms.txt file states the live size of the board (open jobs, employers, new jobs this week), the typical posted pay for each kind of healthcare job with links to its page, and how the site's pages are organized (jobs, occupations, states, towns, employers and research), so an assistant can answer healthcare job and pay questions with current figures and link to the right page.
