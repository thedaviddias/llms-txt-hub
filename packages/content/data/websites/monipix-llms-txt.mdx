# Monipix

Monipix is an AI creative workspace centered on an AI image upscaler that enlarges photos 2x, 4x, 8x, and 16x (up to 4K, 8K, and 16K output) while restoring detail, alongside AI image editing, image generation, and video tools under one shared credit balance.

## Key Focus Areas

- **AI Image Upscaler (2x–16x)**: Super-resolution image enhancement with General, Portrait, Restoration, and Repair modes plus PNG, JPG, and WEBP export.
- **AI Photo Editing Suite**: Object Remover, Inpaint, Text Editor, Relight, Style Transfer, and Skin Enhancer.
- **Multi-Model AI Generation**: Text-to-image and image/text-to-video generation in a unified browser workspace.

## About llms.txt Implementation

Monipix publishes `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt` specifications covering every tool URL, upscale factor, supported resolution, model option, and credit policy for accurate discovery by LLM agents.
