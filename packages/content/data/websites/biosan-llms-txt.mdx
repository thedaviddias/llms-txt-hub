# Biosan

Manufacturer of standard and modified nucleotides, enzymes, DNA markers, monoclonal and polyclonal antibodies, conjugates, and immunosorbents. The company's core specialization is the production of components for mRNA vaccines and mRNA therapeutics: cap analogs, modified NTPs, T7 RNA polymerase, and RNA labeling kits. Products are supplied in research grade and Pharma grade.
