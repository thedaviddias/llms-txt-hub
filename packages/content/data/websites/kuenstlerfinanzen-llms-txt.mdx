# Künstlerfinanzen

Künstlerfinanzen is a web app for artists and writers insured with the German Künstlersozialkasse (KSK). It sets the running profit of the year beside the income estimate the member reported, lists the deadlines of the KSK, the tax office and the collecting societies, and assembles the figures a KSK audit letter asks for. It publishes 26 sourced guides in German and English and open datasets of KSK contribution rates and fixed dates (CC BY 4.0).

## Key Focus Areas

- Künstlersozialkasse income estimate and its 1 December deadline
- KSK random audit (Stichprobenprüfung) and the figures it asks for
- Deadlines of the KSK, the tax office and the collecting societies (VG Wort, VG Bild-Kunst, GEMA)
- Open data: KSK contribution rates and fixed dates, as JSON API and iCalendar feed

## About llms.txt Implementation

The llms.txt is a fact sheet: what the product does and does not do (no legal or tax advice, no filing on the member's behalf), the key dates and rates with their statutory sources, and a link to every guide in German and English. The llms-full.txt contains the full text of all guides.
