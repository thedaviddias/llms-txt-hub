# MRIPrices

Cash-pay MRI prices at 5,419 US hospitals, read from the price files hospitals must publish. Compare real prices by state and city before you book a scan.

## What MRIPrices offers

- **5,400+ hospitals**: state and city pages listing cash-pay MRI prices read from hospitals' mandated machine-readable price files.
- **Price transparency data**: real negotiated and cash prices rather than estimates.
- **MCP server and public API**: read-only tools (`search_listings`, `get_listing`, `list_places`, `list_categories`, `get_stats`, `list_sources`) at `https://mriprices.fyi/mcp` for AI assistants.

## About the llms.txt

The llms.txt describes the price-file data provenance, URL structure (state → city → hospital), and how AI assistants should use the MCP tools to answer MRI-price questions.
