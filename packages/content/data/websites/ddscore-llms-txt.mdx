# DDScore

DDScore provides first-pass due diligence for private companies. It cross-checks pitch decks, business plans, and supporting documents against each other and against public sources.

The report brings together an overall 0–100 score, findings across 12 business dimensions, and follow-up questions. Founders can use it to spot gaps in their fundraising materials, and investors can use it to plan their next diligence questions.

The [llms.txt file](https://www.ddscore.ai/llms.txt) describes the product and points readers to [a public example report](https://www.ddscore.ai/example-report/).
