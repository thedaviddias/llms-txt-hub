# imgmov media utilities

The public imgmov llms.txt index includes links to two standalone media-preparation tools: an image resizer with crop or padding, custom dimensions, and JPEG or PNG output; and a last-frame extractor that exports a PNG from a browser-decodable local video. These utilities run locally without an account.

This entry is limited to those references and utilities. The wider documentation also describes generation models and APIs; their operation was not verified for this submission. The llms-full.txt resource is not included.
