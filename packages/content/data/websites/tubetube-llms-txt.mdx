# TubeTube

AI video generator that turns song lyrics or a short story into a finished multi-scene video: it composes the music or narration, keeps characters consistent across scenes, animates them with Kling 3, Veo 3.1, Seedance 2 or Hailuo 2.3, and edits the result.
