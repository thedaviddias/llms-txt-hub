# ClipTalk

Make finished TikTok, Reels and YouTube videos from a prompt: script, AI visuals, voiceover, captions and music, rendered to a ready\-to\-post MP4\.

## What ClipTalk does

ClipTalk turns a prompt, script or product page into a finished short video for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts: script, AI visuals, voiceover, captions and music, rendered as an MP4.

## Features

- Finished videos from one sentence: script, AI visuals, voiceover, captions and music
- Templates for faceless stories, UGC ads, explainers, quizzes, micro-dramas and music videos
- Remake a viral video's format for your brand from a link
- Edit by asking: cut silences, fix captions, change music, voice, speed or layout
- Works in Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and any MCP client: `https://www.cliptalk.pro/mcp`
- REST API: `https://www.cliptalk.pro/api/v1`
