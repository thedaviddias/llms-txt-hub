# AirPrompter for Business

AirPrompter is a shared library of AI prompts and multi-step workflows for teams. Prompts and workflows are reviewed and signed off before the team runs them, people run them by name from ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Cursor and other MCP clients, and AirPrompter records which workflows each team runs and from which AI tool.

## Key Focus Areas

- Team prompt and workflow library with review and approval
- Playbooks served over MCP to the AI tools a team already uses
- Governance and Assurance checks against AISPA and a team's own Company Policy
- Team pricing and dated, sourced comparisons

## About llms.txt Implementation

AirPrompter for Business provides an `llms.txt` file with AI-readable information about its product, pricing, comparison pages and documentation.
