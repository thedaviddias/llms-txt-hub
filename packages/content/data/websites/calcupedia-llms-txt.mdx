# Calcupedia

2,400\+ free calculators and unit converters\. Embed any of them on your website in one line of code, or use them inside Claude and other AI assistants\.

## What Calcupedia offers

- **2,400+ free calculators and unit converters** across 36 categories: finance, health, math, construction, physics, unit conversion and more. No signup needed to use any of them.
- **Embeddable calculators**: put any calculator on your own website with one line of code (iframe or script tag), styled to match your brand. Works with WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Webflow, Squarespace, Framer, Next.js, React and plain HTML.
- **MCP server for AI assistants**: connect Claude and other MCP clients to `https://calcupedia.io/api/mcp` (Streamable HTTP, no authentication). Read-only tools: `search_calculators`, `run_calculator` and `get_embed_link`. In Claude, answers include a live calculator card.
