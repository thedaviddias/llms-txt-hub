# Planomy

> Planomy is a free, local-first US personal-finance and retirement planner. Its browser-based tools model year-by-year cash flow, federal and state tax, Social Security, Medicare and IRMAA, required minimum distributions, Roth conversions, Monte Carlo outcomes and historical returns without requiring an account.

## Key Focus Areas

- Retirement planner — year-by-year projection of cash flow, tax, Social Security, Medicare and RMDs, run entirely in the browser
- Calculators — 37 focused calculators covering FIRE, Roth conversions, RMDs, IRMAA, take-home pay, debt payoff and college savings
- Guides — 34 plain-English guides to retirement, tax and personal-finance questions
- Tax data — dated, source-attributed IRS, SSA and CMS figures for 2026, also published as free JSON at /data/
- Local-first storage — plan data stays on the user's device unless they choose an account and cloud sync

## About llms.txt Implementation

Planomy publishes both `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt`. Each entry pairs a page
with a one-line statement of the question it answers, so an assistant can route
a specific question — "what is my RMD this year", "which IRMAA bracket am I in",
"Roth or traditional" — to the single page that actually computes it rather than
to a generic landing page. The `/data/` entry additionally points at
machine-readable JSON of current US tax and retirement figures, each value
carrying a citation to the IRS, SSA or CMS publication it came from.
