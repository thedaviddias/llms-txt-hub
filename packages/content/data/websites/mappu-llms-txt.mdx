# Mappu

Mappu is an AI trip planner and walking guide. Travelers plan a trip as an editable day-by-day itinerary on a map, then walk it with audio guides. More than 1,000 audio guides to landmarks and free self-guided walks are published on the web in eight languages. Mappu runs on the web, iOS and Android.

## Key Focus Areas

- AI trip planning with day-by-day itineraries on a map
- Free audio guides to landmarks, written with AI from cited sources
- Self-guided walking routes with route maps and listening times
- Eight languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese and Traditional Chinese

Mappu's llms.txt is a curated index with product facts, what to cite, and how to use Mappu from an assistant through its remote MCP server. The llms-full.txt names every published audio guide, walk, itinerary and localized page.
