# SHMONG TV

Brooklyn video production company filming corporate events, brand videos, weddings, music videos and podcasts across NYC\. All raw footage included\.

## What they film

SHMONG TV is a video production company based in DUMBO, Brooklyn, at 68 Jay St, Ste 201, Brooklyn, NY 11201. The crew shoots and edits corporate events and conferences, brand and product videos, testimonials, weddings, music videos, nightlife, real estate, drone footage and podcasts across New York City, Long Island, Westchester and New Jersey.

## Services

- Corporate event videography, conference and gala filming
- Brand and promo videos, testimonial videos
- Wedding videography
- Music video production
- Podcast production
- Real estate and drone video
- Video editing and live event recap reels

## What a booking includes

Every production includes all the raw footage and a one-minute recap edit, so the client keeps the full rushes instead of paying for them later. Each project is quoted to the job; call (347) 670-2699 or write `contact@shmong.tv` for a free quote, sent within 24 hours. The studio works by appointment, seven days a week.

## Planning a corporate shoot in New York

Before the crew arrives, agree on the run of show, who is on camera, and the shots you cannot miss (stage wide, audience, backstage, sponsor signage). A one-page brief with timings and the room layout keeps the shoot day short and gets better coverage. More on [corporate event videography in New York](https://shmong.tv/services/corporate-events/).
