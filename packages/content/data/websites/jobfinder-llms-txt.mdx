# JobFinder

JobFinder (jobfinder-ai) is an AI agent for job seekers. It finds matching openings, identifies the hiring manager or founder behind each role and sends a tailored email from the job seeker's own inbox, then follows up until they get a reply.

## Key Focus Areas

- AI job matching
- Hiring-manager outreach
- Follow-ups and reply tracking
- Open job listings
- MCP connector for Claude

## About llms.txt Implementation

JobFinder's llms.txt describes how the agent works and links its job listing and guide pages. The llms-full.txt contains the full content of those pages.
