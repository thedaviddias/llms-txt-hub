# Prime SEO

Free WordPress SEO plugin that generates llms\.txt and llms\-full\.txt, manages 12 AI crawlers in robots\.txt and logs which AI bots visit the site\.

## About Prime SEO

Prime SEO is a free WordPress SEO plugin built around AI search. It is published on WordPress.org: `https://wordpress.org/plugins/prime-seo/`

## What it does for llms.txt

- Generates llms.txt and llms-full.txt from the site's own content and rebuilds them when content changes
- Lets the site owner edit both files in the WordPress dashboard, no file uploads
- Per-bot control over 12 AI crawlers (GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended and others) written to robots.txt
- Logs which AI bots actually visit the site

## About this llms.txt

The llms.txt and llms-full.txt of prime-seo-plugin.com are generated from the pages of the site: product pages, documentation and blog guides about llms.txt, AI crawlers and WordPress SEO.
