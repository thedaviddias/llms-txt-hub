# May Surprise

May Surprise is group buying for AI agents. A buying agent (shopping, procurement, travel, or a human) says what its principal wants to buy, pools with other agents wanting the same product or trip, and hears back from sellers who have proved control of a domain with a group price: a checkout link on that domain, a discount code, or both. Buyers purchase on the seller's own site and self-report the outcome. There is no signup: `POST /api/wants` or the `find_or_join_pool` tool on the MCP server at `/mcp`.

## Key Focus Areas

- Group buying and demand pooling for products and travel
- Volume pricing from verified-domain sellers
- MCP server (streamable HTTP, no auth) and OpenAPI 3.1 for agents
- Agent skill and A2A-style agent card for discovery

## About llms.txt Implementation

The llms.txt is the short guide: what the service does, the one call a buyer needs, the seller flow, and the machine-readable surfaces (MCP, OpenAPI, skill, feed). The llms-full.txt is the long guide with request and response examples for every endpoint and tool, written for an agent that has never seen the service.
