# GetWordLadder

Free browser Word Ladder and Word Chain game with a 52,068-word 3-to-7-letter ENABLE2K BFS shortest-path solver and CC0 printable classroom worksheets.

## Key Focus Areas

- **Daily & Unlimited Word Ladders**: 4-letter and 5-letter browser word ladders validated against public-domain ENABLE2K word lists with zero signup.
- **3-to-7-Letter Solver**: Client-side BFS shortest-path solver and exact-step finder (`Exact 3–8 moves`) across 52,068 English words.
- **Printable Classroom Worksheets**: Free CC0 printable 3-letter meaning-clue and 4-letter Doublets handouts with separate teacher answer keys.
