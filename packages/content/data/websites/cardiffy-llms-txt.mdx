# Cardiffy

Cardiffy is a free digital business card app for iPhone, Android and the web\. Share by QR, link or tap, scan paper cards, and keep every contact in one place\.

## What Cardiffy is

A digital business card and link-in-bio page in one, for iPhone, Android and the web. Made by THEORIST LLC.

## Key features

- Seven card styles on a public page with Save contact and Send yours back
- Share by QR code, link, NFC tag, email signature or tap to swap
- Free, offline paper business card scanner
- Contacts Wallet with notes, tags and reminders

## Pricing

Free plan. VIP: $4.99/week, $39.99/year (7-day free trial) or $79.99 lifetime (US prices).
