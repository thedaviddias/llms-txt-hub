# Cost to Drill a Well

Cost to Drill a Well is a public-records cost reference for residential water wells in the U.S. It combines well depth statistics from 35 state agency well record datasets with published driller rates to estimate drilling, pump, pressure tank and casing costs by state and county.

## Key Focus Areas

- Well drilling cost by state and county
- Cost per foot and cost at common well depths
- Pump, pressure tank, casing and water testing costs
- Open well depth dataset (CC BY 4.0, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24149620)

## About llms.txt Implementation

The llms.txt lists the main cost pages, the calculator, the methodology and sources pages, and the open depth dataset, so AI assistants can find the cited figure for a state or cost question.
