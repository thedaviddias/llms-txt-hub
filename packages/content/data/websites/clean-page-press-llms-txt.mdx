# Clean Page Press

Clean Page Press formats books for self-publishing authors who upload their own files to Amazon KDP. Each book gets a Kindle EPUB and a print PDF at its trim size, checked in Kindle Previewer and against KDP print specs. The team also offers editing, ebook covers, cover wraps and KDP listing prep.

## Key Focus Areas

- KDP ebook formatting
- Paperback formatting
- Cover wraps
- KDP listing prep
