# Svoy Agent

Car insurance agent in Moscow: OSAGO and KASKO from 10 insurers and car sale contracts, while your car is washed\.

## What it is

Svoy Agent is an insurance agent at a car wash in Moscow (site in Russian). He arranges compulsory OSAGO and voluntary KASKO car insurance from 10 insurance companies and drafts car sale contracts. An electronic policy can be issued while the car is being washed.
