# Cited Conversations

A reference library of expert answers from recorded human conversations\. Every claim links to the moment it was said\.

## Key Focus Areas

- **Expert Interviews**: In-depth conversations with sales and marketing leaders, operators, and subject-matter experts
- **AI-Ready Answers**: Every episode structured as a retrievable, citable answer library — clear sections, direct quotes, sourced claims
- **Citation-First Publishing**: Content engineered from the ground up so AI answer engines can find, retrieve, and cite it accurately
