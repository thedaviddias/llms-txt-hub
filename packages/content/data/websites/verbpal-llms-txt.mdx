# VerbPal

Learn verb conjugations in real sentences with spaced repetition\. Language\-learning app for 10 languages incl\. Spanish, French and German\. iOS, Android, web\.

## About VerbPal

VerbPal is a language-learning app for mastering verb conjugations in context. Instead of memorizing tables, learners practise verbs inside real sentences, and spaced repetition schedules each review right before they would forget.

## Highlights

- **10 languages:** Spanish, French, Portuguese, English, German, Italian, Polish, Romanian, Dutch and Ukrainian
- Short daily sessions, streaks and progress tracking
- Available on iOS, Android and the web
