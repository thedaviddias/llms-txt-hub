# AEO Footnotes

A free publication about AEO, GEO and AI search where every article cites its sources, with a directory of AEO tools and agencies\.

## About AEO Footnotes

AEO Footnotes is a free, open publication about AI visibility: answer engine optimization (AEO), generative engine optimization (GEO), LLM visibility and AI search across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Google AI Mode. Every article ends with a Footnotes block listing the sources it cites.

## What the llms.txt covers

- Sourced articles and Footnotes Lab research, such as robots.txt and llms.txt across AEO tool and agency sites
- The directory of AEO tools and agencies, by category
- Topic hubs: AEO, GEO, LLM visibility, AI search, AI discovery
- Feeds and the Footnote newsletter

llms-full.txt carries the full text of published articles, regenerated as new pieces publish.
