# Xenition

Xenition is one AI workspace where a single chat creates, edits and searches your documents, slides, spreadsheets, notes, boards, 3D models and forms — replacing a stack of single-purpose tools with one.

## Key Focus Areas

- **One workspace, many surfaces**: Documents, slides, spreadsheets, notes, boards, 3D models and forms, all driven from one chat with shared memory.
- **Build and deploy**: Describe an app and get a working one — database, auth, API, web and mobile front ends, and a live deployment.
- **AI media and automations**: Generate images, video and voice, and run agents and automations with an approval gate.
