# ToyCarToReal

ToyCarToReal turns a single photo of any car into a seamless single-take 9:16 vertical "Toy Car to Real Car" AI transition video (~10s or ~15s MP4 with native engine and road audio) for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.

## Key Focus Areas

- Single-photo miniature train-window to full-size highway AI video generation
- Three transformation scenes (Wet Night Expressway, Sunset Race Circuit, Coastal Cliff Highway)
- Native 9:16 vertical MP4 output in 480p and 768p HD with one-time credit packs and automatic failed-render refunds
