# People Can Build

People Can Build is a Poland-based general contractor for exhibition stands, delivering across Europe with partner coverage in the Middle East, East Asia and the US. We build what agencies design and sell — white-label, strict non-compete — and turn-key stands for direct exhibitors. ~40 projects a year, 16 years in the trade.
