# PrimeReviewsPro

Free fake\-review checker for Amazon product pages, on the web and as a Chrome, Edge and Firefox extension\. Flags suspicious review patterns\.

## What it does

- Reads the reviews on an Amazon product page
- Flags warning signs such as timing clusters, repeated phrasing and odd rating distributions
- 10 free checks a day with no account

A heuristic signal, not proof that a review is fake.
