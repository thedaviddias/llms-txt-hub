# GoalPulse

GoalPulse is an independent football analytics site. It publishes hourly pre-match probabilities for 15 club competitions (1X2, handicap, correct score, total goals and HT/FT) from a Dixon-Coles bivariate Poisson model blended with bookmaker consensus, and keeps a published 6,249-match calibration record. The site is available in 8 languages.

## About llms.txt Implementation

GoalPulse provides an English `llms.txt` that summarises its prediction pipeline, data sources and content boundaries, and links all 8 language editions of the site.
