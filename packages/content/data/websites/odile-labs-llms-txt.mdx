# Odile Labs

Odile Labs makes businesses reachable by AI agents, keeps those agents inside the calls they are allowed to make, and lets the agent pay for each action as it happens.

## Key Focus Areas

- Odile Direct: an AI agent reaches a participating business by its phone number over MCP or A2A; lookups are free and any business can claim its number
- Odile Gate: keys, allowed calls, held approvals and an audit log in front of your own API, as a separate hosted MCP server
- Machine payments: paid routes answer HTTP 402 with x402 payment requirements, so a wallet pays per action with no account
- Phone lines that answer callers

## About llms.txt Implementation

The llms.txt at https://odilelabs.com/llms.txt opens with a one-paragraph summary and a where-to-start block: the MCP endpoints for Direct and Gate, the A2A agent card, the LangChain and LlamaIndex packages and the paid routes. It then lists each product with its prices and limits. An llms-full.txt is published beside it.
