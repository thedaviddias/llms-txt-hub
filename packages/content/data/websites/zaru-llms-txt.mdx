# Zaru

Zaru is a personal AI that remembers you, handles your email, calendar and web research, runs workflows on a schedule, and shows every step as it works. Sending an email or changing your calendar waits for your approval. Settings > Memory shows what Zaru remembers, to read, edit or reset. Free plan, in beta; registering takes a name and an email.

- App: https://ask.myzaru.com
- MCP: connect Zaru to Claude Code, Cursor or any MCP client with `zaru init`
