# Heart Detective

A free educational website that teaches children and families about heart health, diabetes, and cholesterol, in 30 languages. Made for kids to explore with parents, teachers, and grandparents. It teaches three things: how the heart and cholesterol work, what diabetes does and how it is controlled, and the family rule that catches inherited high cholesterol early.
