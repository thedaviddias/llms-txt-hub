# Greadme

Greadme audits a page or a whole site for SEO, performance, accessibility, security and structured data, checks whether AI systems can access and cite your site, and turns every finding into a fix you can hand to your AI coding assistant. Two tools are free with no signup: the Schema Validator and the AI Access Checker.
