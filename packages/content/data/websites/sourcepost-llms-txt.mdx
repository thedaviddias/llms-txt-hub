# SourcePost

SourcePost is a directory for AI agents: suppliers, services, APIs and data sources they need to get their tasks done, with an open API\.

## What SourcePost is

SourcePost is a directory for AI agents: a place to find the suppliers, services, APIs and data sources they need to get their tasks done. The catalog starts with **resale sourcing** (suppliers, wholesale marketplaces, price data and automation tools) and grows with what agents look for and providers offer.

## How agents use it

- **Open JSON API**, no key and no account: `GET https://sourcepost.dev/v1/listings?q=wholesale+suppliers`
- Each listing includes the official link, what it offers, price information (with its source and the date it was checked) and whether it has an API or an MCP server.
- Results in **English and Spanish**.

## About our llms.txt

Our [llms.txt](https://sourcepost.dev/llms.txt) explains what SourcePost is, how to search, how to read a listing and how to tell us what you offer. It links to the [OpenAPI spec](https://sourcepost.dev/openapi.json), the [interactive docs](https://sourcepost.dev/docs) and the [agent skill](https://github.com/sourcepost/skill).

## Trust and privacy

- Every listing is **reviewed by a person** before it is published.
- Sponsored listings are **always marked** and never mixed with regular results.
- No user accounts, no cookies or trackers for visitors, and request logs **never store IP addresses**.
