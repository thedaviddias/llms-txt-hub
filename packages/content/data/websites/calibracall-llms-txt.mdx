# Calibracall

Call QA software in English and Spanish\. The AI scores a criterion alone only once it agrees with the team's reviewers\. Pre\-launch\.

## What Calibracall is

Call QA software for phone teams that review recorded calls against their own scorecard, in English and Spanish. It is pre-launch, with a waitlist open.

## How it will work

- Scores every recorded call on the team's scorecard and quotes the transcript line behind each score.
- A calibration gate: the AI scores a criterion on its own only once it agrees with the team's reviewers at a level the team sets.
- Required-wording checks for collections calls, in English or Spanish, including calls that switch language.
- A free plan of 20 audio hours a month; paid plans priced by audio hour.
