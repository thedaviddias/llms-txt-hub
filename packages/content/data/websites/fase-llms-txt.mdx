# FASE

FASE is a Brazilian web application for designing electrical control panels and motor drive panels. It sizes each motor with the formula and assumptions in view, generates diagrams in the EPLAN sheet pattern (IEC 81346), lays out the panel in 2D and 3D with clearances and collisions, and checks NR-10, NR-12, NBR 5410, IEC 60204-1 and IEC 61439 with evidence and revision history.

## Key Focus Areas

- Motor sizing with visible formulas and standard references
- Schematic sheets following IEC 81346
- 2D/3D panel layout with clearance and collision checks
- NR-12 retrofit panels (safety relay, emergency stop, door sensors)

## About llms.txt Implementation

FASE publishes `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt` (in Portuguese) with the product overview, pricing and the standards it checks.
