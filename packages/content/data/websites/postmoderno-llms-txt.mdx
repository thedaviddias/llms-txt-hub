# postmoderno

Social media scheduler you run from your AI agent\. Connect ChatGPT, Claude, Claude Code or Cursor over MCP, then post to Bluesky, Mastodon, X and LinkedIn\.

## What it does

postmoderno schedules and publishes posts to Bluesky, Mastodon, X and LinkedIn. You tell your AI agent what to post and when. There is no compose box.

## How it works

- **Connect**: point any MCP client at `https://mcp.postmoderno.io/mcp`. ChatGPT, claude.ai, Claude Code and Codex sign in with OAuth.
- **Preview first**: your agent shows the post with your local time and UTC, and nothing is scheduled until you confirm.
- **Publish**: failed attempts are retried, and you see what happened on each platform.
