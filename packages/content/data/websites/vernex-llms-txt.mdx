# Vernex

Vernex is a managed marketplace for custom wire harnesses, cable assemblies and control panel wiring, aimed at hardware engineers, robotics and industrial-machinery OEMs, and hardware startups across Europe.

## How it works

Upload a drawing (PDF, DXF, STEP), a BOM, an EPLAN or Zuken E3 export, a photo of the harness being replaced, or just a description. A fixed price comes back within 24 hours. Production starts from 10 days. Vernex is the single counterparty for quote, quality and delivery — whichever partner physically builds the harness.

## What it builds

Wire harnesses, cable assemblies (including RF/coaxial, ribbon/IDC, HV/battery), control-panel wiring. No minimum order quantity. Serves OEM & electronics, robotics, industrial machinery, energy, railroad and medical sectors.
