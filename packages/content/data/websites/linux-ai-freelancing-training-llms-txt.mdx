# School Of Freelancing

School Of Freelancing is a Bangladesh-based platform, founded in 2015 by Karim (Masum), that teaches Linux and AI freelancing through live, instructor-led training. It also provides professional technical support to freelancers, startups, and businesses worldwide. The site is hosted on Cloudflare and is Agent Ready.

## Key Focus Areas

- Linux Freelancing Training: Linux, Ubuntu, Debian, CentOS, Docker, GitHub, GitLab
- AI & Agents Training: Hermes Agent, OpenClaw, ZeroClaw, LocalAI, Odysseus AI, OpenAI, Claude, Voice AI
- Communications & VoIP: FusionPBX, GOautodial, call centers, Telnyx, Twilio, bulk SMS and SMS gateways
- Client Support: 24/7 cloud and server support, DigitalOcean, Hummingbot, AI infrastructure setup
