# TimesheetTab

TimesheetTab is a free, client-side US work hours, payroll timesheet, and punch time card calculator suite that computes daily totals, unpaid lunch deductions, overnight shifts, FLSA weekly overtime, and gross pay directly in the browser without sign-up or server uploads.

## Key Focus Areas

- Weekly (7-day) and biweekly (14-day) payroll timesheet and multi-row punch time card calculators with `localStorage` draft saving and `#rate` URL fragment privacy
- Focused browser utilities including a 7-Minute Rule rounding calculator (`29 CFR § 785.48(b)`), Decimal Hours converter, Time-and-a-Half / double-time calculator, and Shift Differential weighted overtime calculator
- Direct export to CSV, native Excel (`.xlsx`) workbooks, and printable sheets with signature lines

TimesheetTab's `llms.txt` indexes both flagship exportable timesheets, all four focused payroll reference calculators, FLSA calculation scope notes, and methodology/privacy documentation.
