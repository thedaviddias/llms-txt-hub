# AUGMANITAI

Independent research on performance, meaning and judgment in human-machine collaboration. The public lexicon gives precise names to the small moments between people and AI. The machine-readable layer adds schema.org and SKOS serialisations, an OAI-PMH harvesting endpoint and persistent identifiers.
