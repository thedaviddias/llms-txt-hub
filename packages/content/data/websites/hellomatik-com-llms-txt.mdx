# Hellomatik

Hellomatik integrates the data from a company's systems (ERP, CRM, warehouse management system and email) into a single blueprint, for one department or the whole organisation. AI agents operate on that blueprint: they answer business questions, send alerts and prepare each operation, and the company decides which operations complete automatically and which wait for an approver to accept, amend or reject.
