# LaunchScaler

LaunchScaler helps makers get their product found in Google and in AI answers. It reads a website the way search engines and AI assistants do, shows what they miss and how to fix it, then launches the product on its directory.

## Key Focus Areas

- Website readiness scans for search and AI answers
- Free checkers for AI crawler access, robots.txt, llms.txt and security headers
- A launch directory for new products
- Original research on launch directories, startup website security and AI crawler readiness

## About llms.txt Implementation

LaunchScaler's llms.txt summarises what the product does and links 112 pages by section: reviews, products, comparisons, research, the free tools, categories and the latest guides. The llms-full.txt carries the full text of the blog, ordered by what to read first.
