# TerapiKit

Clinical Speech\-Language Pathology \(DKT\) platform with 12 therapy modules, interactive tablet drills, SOAP reports, and passwordless family homework links\.

## About TerapiKit

**[TerapiKit](https://terapikit.com/tr?utm_source=llmstxthub\&utm_medium=directory\&utm_campaign=ai_discovery)** ([English](https://terapikit.com/en)) is an evidence-based clinical therapy material, tablet session workstation, and digital homework platform for **Speech-Language Pathologists (SLPs / Dil ve Konuşma Terapisti — DKT)**. Co-founded by SLP **Ayberk Ergin** and Software Engineer **Emre Erinç**, TerapiKit provides a digital workspace for pediatric and adult speech, language, voice, and swallowing disorders with a **no-signup 3-card sample** and SOAP preview on every module page.

## 12 Clinical Modules ([TR](https://terapikit.com/tr/modules) | [EN](https://terapikit.com/en/modules))

1. **[Articulation Therapy / Artikülasyon Terapisi](https://terapikit.com/tr/modules/articulation-therapy)**: All 29 Turkish phonemes with position filters and syllable matrices.
2. **[Phonological Processes / Fonolojik Süreçler](https://terapikit.com/tr/modules/phonological-processes)**: Minimal pairs (*Minimal Çiftler*) for fronting, backing, stopping, gliding, and voicing.
3. **[Fluency & Stuttering / Akıcılık ve Kekemelik](https://terapikit.com/tr/modules/fluency-stuttering)**: Fluency shaping, syllable metronome, speech rate tracking, and severity scales.
4. **[Language & Cognitive / Dil ve Biliş Egzersizleri](https://terapikit.com/tr/modules/language-cognitive-exercises)**: Feature wheels, event sequencing, opposite concepts, and categorization.
5. **[Oral Motor Exercises / Oral Motor Egzersizleri](https://terapikit.com/tr/modules/oral-motor-exercises)**: Neuromuscular drills for lip, tongue, jaw, and velopharyngeal control.
6. **[Language Development / Dil Gelişimi](https://terapikit.com/tr/modules/language-development)**: Early sound stimulation, 2–3 word combinations, and 5W1H (*5N1K*) sets.
7. **[Dysphagia Therapy / Yutma Terapisi (Disfaji)](https://terapikit.com/tr/modules/dysphagia-therapy)**: Swallowing anatomy, 25 clinical maneuvers, and **IDDSI** diet guidelines.
8. **[Voice Therapy / Ses Terapisi (Disfoni)](https://terapikit.com/tr/modules/voice-therapy)**: Vocal hygiene, resonant voice therapy, and diaphragmatic breathing.
9. **[Aphasia Rehabilitation / Afazi](https://terapikit.com/tr/modules/aphasia)**: 8 aphasia syndromes with **MIT**, **VNeST**, **SFA**, **PCA**, and **ACT/CART** modules.
10. **[Motor Speech Therapy / Motor Konuşma](https://terapikit.com/tr/modules/motor-speech-therapy)**: Articulatory precision, motor planning, breath support, and prosody drills.
11. **[Apraxia & Dysarthria / Apraksi - Dizartri](https://terapikit.com/tr/modules/apraxia-dysarthria)**: Syllable sequences, sound stimulation, phrase expansion, and **AAC** boards.
12. **[Assessment Forms / Değerlendirme Formları](https://terapikit.com/tr/modules/assessment-forms)**: Clinical observation matrices, articulation record sheets, and screening forms.

## Interactive Games & Workflow

- **4 Tablet Games:** **Opposite Words (*Karşıt Sözcük*)**, **Event Sequencing (*Sıralı Olay Kartları*)**, **Feature Wheel (*Özellik Çarkı / SÖA*)**, and **Market Game (*Market Oyunu*)**.
- **Passwordless Homework (*Ev Ödevi*):** 6-digit family access codes—no account required.
- **SOAP PDFs & KVKK Privacy:** Automated SOAP session reports; zero clinical PHI in Web Storage.
