# VI Font Studio

Make an original GTA VI-inspired title, logo or cover. Preview six Miami Deco styles live, then export a 2x PNG or SVG without uploading anything.

## Key Focus Areas

- Real-time HTML5 Canvas typography studio with six Miami Deco style presets and 2x PNG/SVG export
- Browser-local logo rendering and typography prompt workbench requiring no signup or upload
- Seven localized editions (en, es, pt-BR, fr, de, ja, zh-CN) with export specification tables
