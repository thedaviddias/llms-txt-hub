# 养鲜家 · 药食同源数智平台 (Yangxianjia)

An AI-native public platform for Chinese "food-medicine homology" (药食同源) knowledge:

- **106 herbs** from the national catalogue *Substances Traditionally Both Food and Chinese Medicine*, one profile page per herb
- **Geographic indication** products and **Dao-di** (geo-authentic) producing regions across 30 provinces
- **Industry research reports** in five cadences (daily / weekly / monthly / quarterly / yearly), auto-compiled from authoritative sources and reviewed
- **Industry directories**: associations, GI holders, herb catalogue, Dao-di catalogues, verified producers

Content is for learning, research and science popularisation only; it is not medical or commercial advice.
