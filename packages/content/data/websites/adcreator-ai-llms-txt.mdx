# AdCreator AI

Shopify app that turns a product and its photo into ad copy for Facebook, Instagram, Google and TikTok, square image ads and short video ads\.

## What it does

- Writes ad copy separately for Facebook, Instagram, Google and TikTok
- Composites 1080x1080 image ads from the product photo
- 5-30 second video ads with an AI voiceover (Pro)
- Runs inside Shopify admin, including via Shopify Sidekick

Available on the Shopify App Store.
