# LoadOptimizer\.ai

Browser\-based load planning software that calculates how cargo fits into containers, trucks and pallets in 3D, with axle weight and centre of gravity checks\.

## What LoadOptimizer.ai does

LoadOptimizer.ai is browser-based load planning software. You enter or import your cargo list (sizes, weights, quantities and stacking rules), choose containers, trucks or pallets, and get a 3D load plan that shows where every item goes.

## Key features

- **3D load plans** for 20ft, 40ft and high cube containers, trucks, trailers and pallets
- **Axle weight and centre of gravity** checks on every plan
- **Excel and CSV import**, with PDF and Excel export for the loading crew
- **Multi-vehicle planning** to compare carriers in one optimization
- **REST API** for ERP, TMS and WMS integrations (Pro and Business plans)
