# Kuro

Kuro is an AI picture studio for creating images, video clips, voice-overs, music and editable storyboard films. Its hosted MCP server lets AI assistants quote work, generate media and retrieve saved results.

## Key Focus Areas

- AI image and video generation
- Voice-overs and music
- Editable storyboard films
- Reusable creative recipes
- Hosted MCP access with OAuth
