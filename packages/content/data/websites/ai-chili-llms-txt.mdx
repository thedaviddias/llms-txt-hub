# AI Chili

AI Chili is a cinematic AI video generation platform offering text-to-video, image-to-video, first-last-frame conditioning, and talking avatars powered by Chili 2.0 up to 1080p and 4K upscale.

## Key Focus Areas

- **Cinematic Video Generation**: Multi-shot storyboards, character-consistent scenes, and talking-head avatars with web studio and `chili_sk_` REST API access.
- **Direct Streaming Distribution**: Native one-click publishing from AI Chili Studio into OnlyChili Cinema (`https://onlychili.aichili.ai`) for creator subscriptions and pay-per-view series.
