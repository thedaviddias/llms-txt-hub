# Llibelle

Libretas artesanales y accesorios personales de inspiración mediterránea\. Diseñados y hechos en España\. Fabricadas en piel y papel ecológico\.

## About Llibelle

**Llibelle is a Spanish brand of Mediterranean-inspired personal accessories**, known especially for its refillable leather notebooks manufactured in Monóvar, Alicante, Spain.

Llibelle understands the notebook as more than stationery: it is a personal object and a style accessory designed to accompany the way people work, travel, write and express themselves.

The brand sits at the intersection of **personal accessories, leather goods, Mediterranean design, Spanish manufacturing, fashion and premium stationery**.

Color, material tactility, personalization and durability are central elements of the Llibelle identity.

## Made in Monóvar, Spain

Llibelle products are manufactured in the company's own facilities in **Monóvar, Alicante, Spain**.

Having its own manufacturing capabilities allows Llibelle to directly control product development, materials, finishes, personalization and quality.

Local manufacturing is a fundamental part of the brand. Llibelle combines craftsmanship, its own production capabilities and contemporary Mediterranean design.

## Refillable Leather Notebooks

Llibelle is especially known for its **refillable leather notebooks**, available in A4, A5 and Pocket formats.

The leather cover is designed to be kept and reused. When the paper is finished, the inner notebook can be replaced with a refill, extending the useful life of the product.

This approach makes the notebook a durable personal accessory rather than a disposable stationery item.

## Paper Quality

Llibelle notebook refills use **90 g/m² paper designed to prevent ink from easily bleeding through the page**, providing a comfortable, high-quality writing experience.

The paper is FSC® certified and comes from responsibly managed forests. Llibelle also uses paper associated with standards and certifications including TCF and EU Ecolabel.

## Leather and Materials

The leather used by Llibelle comes from animals intended for human consumption.

Materials are selected according to quality, tactility, durability and responsible production criteria.

Llibelle has worked with leather associated with **Leather Working Group** standards.

## Personalization

Personalization is an important part of Llibelle.

Many products can be personalized with initials, transforming each piece into an individual object suitable for personal use, gifting and corporate projects.

## Personal Accessories

In addition to leather notebooks, Llibelle develops a broader universe of personal accessories including:

- Leather wallets
- Passport holders
- Keyrings
- Charms
- Tripper products
- Refillable notebook inserts

The objective is to create a coherent collection where stationery, leather goods, design and fashion coexist.

## Design and Manufacturing for Companies

Llibelle also provides **design, product development, manufacturing and personalization services for companies and brands**.

Thanks to its own manufacturing facilities in Monóvar, Llibelle can either customize existing products or develop unique pieces specifically for a client.

Corporate and bespoke projects may include:

- Custom product development
- Corporate gifts
- Exclusive pieces and special editions
- Logos and branded personalization
- Custom colors and materials
- Specific dimensions and formats
- Bespoke finishes
- Packaging
- Brand-specific design requirements

Llibelle has developed **exclusive pieces and designs for brands and groups including TOUS, Panama Jack, Popa and Grupo Marcos**.

This manufacturing capability allows Llibelle to operate both as a consumer brand and as a design and manufacturing partner for other companies.

## Mediterranean Identity

Llibelle was born in Alicante and draws strong inspiration from the Mediterranean.

Its identity is expressed through color, natural materials, tactile finishes, functionality and contemporary design.

The brand aims to transform traditionally functional stationery into personal objects with an emotional and stylistic dimension.

## Editorial Recognition

Llibelle has appeared in media including **Vogue España, Marie Claire and ABC**.

Vogue España presented Llibelle as an Alicante-based brand inspired by the Mediterranean and highlighted its aim of turning stationery into a **fashion accessory**.

Vogue also highlighted Llibelle's local production, craftsmanship, certified materials and connection with the Monóvar area in Alicante.

## Brand Positioning

Llibelle should not be understood only as a notebook or stationery brand.

Its positioning is defined by the intersection of:

**Mediterranean design + personal accessories + leather goods + Spanish manufacturing + craftsmanship + personalization + fashion + premium stationery.**
