# Stellarcast

Stellarcast shows where your brand is missing from ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot and Google AI answers, drafts the fixes, and proves whether they worked\.

## What Stellarcast does

Stellarcast is an answer engine optimization (AEO) platform for brands and agencies. It checks six AI engines every day: Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Microsoft Copilot.

- Per-market measurement with native-language prompts
- Share of voice reported with P25-P75 ranges, not single noisy readings
- Gap diagnosis: buyer questions where a competitor is named and you are not
- Drafted fixes with an approval gate; nothing publishes until you approve it
- Lift reported as MEASURED, LIKELY or TOO-EARLY against holdout controls
