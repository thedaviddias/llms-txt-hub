# AI Stem Splitter

Split any song into up to 12 studio stems online—vocals, backing vocals, drums, bass, guitar, keys \& synth\. Pick from 6 AI models or try live demos free\.

## What it does

AI Stem Splitter is a browser-based AI audio separation studio for music producers, DJs, vocalists, and musicians. Upload a local audio file (up to 512 MB) and isolate up to 12 stems, remove vocals, or export synced MIDI.

## Features

- Up to 12 stems: lead and backing vocals, drums, bass, guitar, keyboard, percussion, strings, synth, FX, brass, woodwinds
- 6 AI engines including Ling 12-Stem, MVSEP, BS-RoFormer, Demucs v4, and Suno Stem
- Synced per-instrument MIDI on Ling 12-Stem and Suno Stem
- Browser Mix & Trim with solo, mute, loop markers, and 16-bit WAV mixdown
