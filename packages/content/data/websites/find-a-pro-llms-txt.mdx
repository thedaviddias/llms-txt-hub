# Find a Pro

Home\-service pros matched to TX license and business records before they're listed and My Home: your house's record, coverage, bills and storm radar in one app\.

## What Find a Pro is

**Find a Pro** (findaprohub.com) helps Texas homeowners hire a home-service pro they can trust, then keeps track of the house. About 26,000 pros across 34 kinds of work in 20 Texas metro areas, each matched to state records before it's listed. Made by ServicePros Hub LLC in San Antonio. In English and Spanish.

## Key Focus Areas

- **Checked pros**: Every listing is matched to a Texas license file (A/C, electrical, plumbing, pest control and more) or an active Texas business registration, and rechecked on the state's schedule. Each listing shows what was checked and where it came from.
- **Fair ranking**: Nobody pays to be listed or to rank, and pros never pay per lead. Star ratings from other sites never count. See [How we rank](https://findaprohub.com/how-we-rank).
- **AI assistant (Hank)**: Calls up to 3 pros at once (only pros who agreed to his calls) and sends notes from every call, lines up written quotes side by side, and reads your home warranty and insurance policy to show what's likely covered.
- **My Home**: Your house's record, a maintenance calendar with reminders, bills and usage, and storm radar with National Weather Service warnings for your address.

## Plans

- **Free**: Find a checked pro, one saved home, 3 saved pros, severe-storm alerts and the calculators.
- **Premium**: $6.99 a month or $59 a year.
- **Family plan**: $9.99 a month or $89 a year for up to 5 people, each with their own home.
