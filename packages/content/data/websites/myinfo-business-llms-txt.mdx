# myinfo\-business

MyInfo\.lk is a Sri Lankan AI Business Knowledge Platform that provides structured, verified and machine\-readable business information\.

## About MyInfo.lk

MyInfo.lk is a Sri Lankan AI Business Knowledge Platform designed to make structured, verified and AI-readable business information more accessible to modern search engines, AI systems and AI-powered applications.

MyInfo goes beyond a traditional business directory by organizing Sri Lankan business information into structured digital business profiles that can be discovered, understood and processed by AI systems.

## Key Focus Areas

- **AI Business Optimization**: Helping businesses present complete, structured and machine-readable information for the AI search ecosystem.
- **AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)**: Structuring business information to improve its usefulness for answer engines and conversational search.
- **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)**: Preparing business information for the evolving generative AI search environment.
- **AI-Readable Business Data**: Structured information covering business identity, categories, services, locations, service areas, contact information and other relevant business facts.
- **Business Verification & Trust**: Structured verification information and trust signals that provide additional context about a business.
- **Customer Reviews & Review Trust**: Structured customer review information and review trust signals.
- **Local Business Discovery**: Helping users discover relevant businesses based on services, categories and geographic locations across Sri Lanka.
- **Multilingual Business Information**: Supporting business information in Sinhala, Tamil and English contexts.
- **AI and Developer Access**: Providing machine-readable resources, REST APIs and OpenAPI documentation for structured business information.

## Business Knowledge Structure

MyInfo business profiles can contain structured information including:

- Business name and identity
- Business type
- Categories and subcategories
- Products and services
- Primary business location
- Delivery and service areas
- Province, district and town information
- Contact information
- Website and social profiles
- WhatsApp contact information
- Opening hours
- Payment methods
- Business registration information
- Verification information
- Customer reviews and trust information
- Business-specific structured data
- AEO/GEO business information
- AI recommendation-related business facts

## AI-Readable Infrastructure

MyInfo provides machine-readable resources intended for AI systems, developers and search technologies.

The platform includes:

- llms.txt
- llms-full.txt
- REST API
- OpenAPI specification
- Business discovery API
- Business entity information
- Category and taxonomy information
- Location information
- Business type information
- Review information
- AI-oriented business knowledge endpoints

The infrastructure is designed to support scalable discovery of Sri Lankan businesses rather than requiring an AI system to process an entire business dataset at once.

## Business Discovery

MyInfo supports structured discovery using business categories, locations, business types, search queries and other business attributes.

The platform is designed to help AI systems retrieve relevant businesses based on the user's intent, service requirements and geographic needs.

Business relevance and business trust are treated as separate concepts. A business being verified does not automatically mean it is the most relevant result for every query.

## Verification and Trust

Where available, MyInfo can provide structured verification information such as verification status, verification level, certificate information, verification dates and checked business information.

Customer reviews can also contain structured trust information.

Verification and review information are intended to provide evidence and context rather than guarantee a business recommendation by any third-party AI system.

## Vision

MyInfo's long-term vision is to build Sri Lanka's structured, verified and AI-readable business knowledge infrastructure.

The platform aims to connect Sri Lankan businesses, customers, search technologies, developers and AI systems through reliable and structured business information.

MyInfo does not control or guarantee rankings or recommendations made by ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Grok, Copilot or other third-party AI systems. These systems independently determine which sources and businesses they use.

MyInfo's goal is to provide businesses with a strong foundation of accurate, structured, accessible and AI-readable information for the evolving search and AI ecosystem.
