# Quote and Sign

Quote and Sign is open-source (AGPL-3.0) proposal and quote software for agencies, freelancers and service businesses. A proposal is a link instead of a PDF: the client switches optional lines on or off, watches the total update, types their name and taps Accept, and both sides get a signed PDF with a SHA-256 fingerprint of what was accepted. Pricing is flat per account rather than per user, with a free plan.

## Key Focus Areas

- Proposals and quotes with live, client-adjustable pricing
- Electronic acceptance with a verifiable signing record
- Templates for design, marketing, web, consulting, trades and other services
- Self-hostable open-source code
