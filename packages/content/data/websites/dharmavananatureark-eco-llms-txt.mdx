# Dharmavana Nature Ark

Dharmavana Nature Ark (DNA) is a 400+ acre biodiversity conservation ecosystem situated northeast of Hyderabad in Yadadri-Bhuvanagiri District, Telangana. Established in 2005, it conserves rare, endangered and threatened (RET) flora of the Deccan Plateau through ecosystem restoration, silvifera, ad situm conservation, and germplasm preservation.
