# CEO Stealth

A free comedy stealth game that runs in a web browser. You play the elderly CEO of a fictional chip company in a brown leather jacket and walk your date to the limo without being seen by paparazzi, reporters, fans and cameras. Eight short missions, a daily mission, nothing to install. A parody: every person and company in it is fictional.
