# Chain of Thought

Chain of Thought is a podcast hosted by Conor Bronsdon about building AI systems in production, with guests from teams such as Intercom, Cisco, NVIDIA and Thomson Reuters. Each episode page carries a full transcript, chapters and show notes.

The llms.txt file indexes every episode, explainer and glossary entry, and llms-full.txt expands each episode with its summary and key takeaways. The site also publishes a machine-readable corpus and a read-only MCP server, documented at https://chainofthought.show/for-agents/.
