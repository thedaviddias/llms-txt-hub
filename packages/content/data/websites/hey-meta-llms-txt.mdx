# Hey Meta

Free check of how your website looks when shared and whether AI search can read it. Preview link cards on ten platforms, check AI crawler access and copy the fixes.

## Key Focus Areas

- **Link previews**: Shows how a link card looks on Facebook, X, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Google, Slack, Discord, Telegram, Microsoft Teams and iMessage, and flags missing or oversized titles, descriptions and images.
- **AI readiness**: Scores whether ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity can reach and quote a page: AI crawler access in robots.txt, firewall blocks, noindex, text readable without JavaScript, and quotable content.
- **Fixes**: Generates meta tags as HTML, Next.js or Nuxt code, plus robots.txt rules, JSON-LD and an llms.txt draft.

## About llms.txt Implementation

Our llms.txt describes each tool and links the guides for fixing link previews per platform, the link preview cheat sheet and the AI crawler guides.
