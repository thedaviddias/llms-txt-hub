# CueCheck

CueCheck is a free, client-side subtitle quality control (QC) suite for SRT and WebVTT files that audits reading speed (CPS and WPM), characters per line, cue duration, 2-frame inter-cue gaps, and overlapping timings directly in the browser without uploading files to a server.

## Key Focus Areas

- Built-in broadcast and streaming presets verified against Netflix English Timed Text (20/17 CPS, 42 chars/line, 2-frame gap), BBC Subtitle Guidelines (160–180 WPM, 37 chars/line), and DCMP Open Captions (32 chars/line)
- Interactive SRT/WebVTT QC workbench, CPS/WPM calculators, and YouTube caption checker
- NDA-safe client-side parsing with cue-by-cue CSV QC report export and zero registration required
