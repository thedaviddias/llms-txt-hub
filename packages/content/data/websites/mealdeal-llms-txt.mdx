# MealDeal

MealDeal is a French-language (fr-CA) web app for Quebec households. It plans lunch and dinner for seven days around the week's grocery flyer deals for a Canadian postal code, then produces a grocery list split by store.

## Key Focus Areas

- Meal planning from local flyer deals
- Per-store grocery lists
- Pantry tracking, dietary restrictions and allergies
- Installable web app (PWA) with offline grocery list
