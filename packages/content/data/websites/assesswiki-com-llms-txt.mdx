# AssessWiki

AssessWiki is an independent assessment library and application directory. It curates evidence-based assessment frameworks — Big Five/OCEAN, conflict styles, leadership, career values, flow, burnout — and documents each tool in a fixed structure: what it measures, who it is for, how it works, what evidence supports it, what it cannot tell you, and what to do next.

Every entry carries a dated review line, a public corrections log records what was wrong and when, and a reference index lists the 88 published works the library rests on. Reviews are independent: no payment for placement, reviews or inclusion. All assessments are free to take, with no sign-up.
