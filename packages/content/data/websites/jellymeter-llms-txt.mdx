# Jellymeter

Jellymeter is a free public service that estimates the risk of meeting jellyfish on 195 beaches in Thailand today and for the next 5 days. The estimate combines official DMCR warnings, wind, wave and current forecasts and beachgoer reports. Available in English, Russian and Thai.
